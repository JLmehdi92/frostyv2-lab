Latency

Seconds, not tens of seconds.

Embedded web players keep a deep buffer and fall well behind the broadcast. ONSET's native player, built on Amazon IVS, the same video technology as Twitch, keeps you close to the edge.

  1. 01

    Live happens at the edge.

    The broadcast is written at its newest moment. Everything behind that edge is already the past.

  2. 02

    Web embeds wait in the past.

    A web player embed builds a long buffer, so what you watch trails what is happening.

  3. 03

    ONSET rides the edge.

    A short buffer, a smooth catch-up when it drifts, and the measured delay shown in the player.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Write to contact@onsetapp.app.

Is ONSET free?

Yes. ONSET is free to download and use, and it is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license.

Is it an official Twitch app?

No. ONSET is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Twitch.

Do I need a Twitch account?

No. You can browse and watch while logged out. To chat or see the channels you follow, you log in on Twitch's own sign-in page.

Does ONSET collect my data?

No ads, no data sold; analytics stay off unless you opt in.

How do I install the APK?
  1. Download the APK.
  2. Open it, and allow your browser to install apps when Android asks.
  3. Open ONSET.

After that, updates arrive inside the app.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Join the iPhone waitlist to hear when it is ready.

What do I need to run it?

A 64-bit Android device running Android 7.0 or later.
