## v1.0.0

October 29, 2026

The first ONSET: a new design on phone and tablet, and live video much closer to live.

### New

- [Android] **Low-latency player:** streams play with the Amazon IVS player, built on the same video technology as Twitch, and the latency shows in the player.
  - Turn it off in `Settings › Video` to use the Twitch web player.
- **Noir & Signal:** every screen redesigned, with its own layout on tablets.
- Home, Browse, Activity and Profile in one navigation bar, with Following, Live and Clips on Home.
- A first-launch setup: sign in with Twitch, then pick a theme and an accent colour.
- Raids: an incoming raid is pinned above chat, and an outgoing raid shows where the channel is heading.

### Improvements

- **Celebrations on video** in `Settings › Chat`: play them over the video, or keep it clear with a line in chat.
- Settings were reorganised: Notifications has its own page, and About ends Other.

### Fixes

- On tablets, the video takes the whole column again when the info area under it has no room.
