## v1.1.0

November 1, 2026

ONSET now speaks French, and the chat keyboard opens smoothly.

### New

- **French:** the whole app is available in French. Pick English or French on first launch, or anytime in `Settings › General`.

### Improvements

- The keyboard now slides up smoothly when you tap the chat field, with less work for the app on every frame.
- Clearer, more consistent English across the app.

### Fixes

- Tapping a name on some older chat lines no longer closes the app.

## v1.0.0

October 29, 2026

The first release of ONSET: a new design for phones and tablets, and live video with far less delay.

### New

- [Android] **Low-latency player:** streams play in the Amazon IVS player, built on the same video technology as Twitch, and the latency shows in the player.
  - Turn it off in `Settings › Video` to use the Twitch web player.
- **Noir & Signal:** every screen redesigned, with its own layout on tablets.
- Home, Browse, Activity, and Profile in one navigation bar, with Following, Live, and Clips on Home.
- A first-launch setup: log in with Twitch, then pick a theme and an accent color.
- Raids: an incoming raid is pinned above chat, and an outgoing raid shows where the channel is heading.

### Improvements

- **Celebrations on video** in `Settings › Chat`: play them over the video, or keep it clear with a line in chat.
- Settings were reorganized: Notifications has its own page, and About is now at the end of Other.

### Fixes

- On tablets, the video takes the whole column again when the info area under it has no room.
