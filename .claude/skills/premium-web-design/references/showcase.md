## 5. Recurring patterns (synthesis across all of the above)
- **One signature element per page**: particle field, 3D ribbon, VHS spines, floating island + scroll zoom, clothing rail, self-drawing bike, curved card carousel, ASCII field.
- **Type at poster scale**, tight tracking, often cropped or overlapping; paired with a **mono utility face** for labels/data.
- **Restrained palette**: a base (black, off-white, misty grey) plus one loud accent; secondary accents only in small doses.
- **Real-looking assets** (photoreal products, people, islands) rather than flat gradients.
- **Data micro-copy** (coordinates, specs, timecodes, "01/03") gives an instrument/editorial feel.
- **Scroll as narrative**: pinned sections, zoom-ins, scene changes.
- **Interaction rewards hover**: objects pull forward, fields react to the cursor.
