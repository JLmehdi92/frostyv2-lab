## Purpose

ONSET exists to watch Twitch live streams with drastically lower latency than the Twitch web embed (target 2–4 s, official-app level, versus 8–15 s) while keeping every feature. This leaf is the screen where that happens: `VideoChat` (route `'VideoChat'`), the live channel screen that combines a video player and the channel's chat, plus everything drawn over or around the video.

Responsibilities:
- Choose and run one of three player engines behind one `PlayerController` contract: Amazon IVS native player (default, Android; renders into a Flutter texture so it can move to the in-app mini player without reloading), media_kit/libmpv native HLS (fallback), and the Twitch web player in a WebView (fallback of last resort, always launchable). Any native failure that is not a confirmed offline channel falls back to the web engine for the session, telling the viewer when Twitch refused the stream.
- Keep latency low and visible: IVS low-latency tuning (100 ms initial buffer, optional Ultra buffer 0.5 s / ceiling 3.0 s), the Dart "auto latency" controller that plays at 1.05x to drain drift (never reloading), an SDK-like 1.03x drain fallback, a live-edge guard that reloads the stream only when drift stays > max(5 s, floor + 2.5 s) for 10 s, resume-at-live after a pause >= 10 s, an Ultra -> Standard 30-minute fallback after two stalls in 60 s, and a latency pill always visible over the video (project rule: latency must be measurable on a real device) with a stats panel (median/p90/max over 5 minutes, buffer, bitrate, frames, speed, auto-latency phase).
- Sync chat delay to the measured latency (whole seconds, with hysteresis under auto latency).
- Recover from errors with a bounded policy (1 s / 2 s / 4 s in a 60 s window, fresh token on 401/403, Helix-confirmed offline state, polling + EventSub to restart when live).
- Provide every live interaction: controls overlay (auto-hide 5 s), quality menu (with unavailable renditions and bitrates), volume, captions (tracks, size, background), clip creation, share, cast, pop-out floating player, picture-in-picture, swipe-down-to-mini-player, pinch to fill, double tap to cycle landscape chat layouts, fullscreen/rotation, resizable portrait split, resizable side chat, overlay chat, tablet info area with Follow/Subscribe and "also live", followed-channels drawer, mature-content warning (own and Twitch's), free preview countdown, stream-end auto-continue into the VOD, live progress saved for later VOD resume, raids (go along / arrival), creator goals, follow prompts, Hermes real-time channel events.

## Screens and layouts

All layouts are one widget tree that switches arrangement while keeping the player, the chat and the content warning mounted through `GlobalKey`s (`_videoKey`, `_chatKey`, `_contentGateKey`): rotating never reloads the video, never uncovers a covered stream and never re-asks Twitch for labels.

1. **Portrait phone** (`!isLargeScreen`, portrait): `SafeArea(top: showVideo, bottom: false)`; a column with a placeholder of the video height (`portraitVideoHeight(width, scale)` = width x 9/16 x scale), the `PortraitSplitHandle` (14 px, pill 36x4), the `ChannelInfoRow` (avatar 40 in the live ring, name 15 w700, "category · 2,445 watching · 3 h 12", Follow 36 high), then chat; the video itself is positioned over the placeholder, centred, width `videoHeight x 16/9`, wrapped in the swipe-down gesture. Video scale presets from `PortraitChatHeight` (low 1.0, medium halfway, high minimum, custom dragged), minimum `max(0.4, 300/width)` (1.0 when width <= 300). Sheets open under the video (`SheetsUnderVideo(top: safeTop + videoHeight)`) so the live and its latency stay visible. While the keyboard is up the info row folds away (250 ms out / 350 ms in).
2. **Portrait tablet** (large, portrait): same column; the `ChannelInfoRow` is the large variant (avatar 48, ring 2, name 18 w800, "title — category", Follow 40 + Subscribe), the overlay hides its identity (`showIdentity = false`) because the row under the video has it; never folded while typing.
3. **Landscape phone, side column** (`landscapeChatMode == column`, i.e. `fullScreen == false`): `SafeArea(bottom: false, left/right respecting the "landscape cutout" setting)`; `Row[video Expanded, chat AnimatedContainer(width = availableWidth x chatWidth (default 0.2) or 0.5 when the chat is expanded)]` (mirrored when `landscapeChatLeftSide`), a `DraggableDivider` (min 0.2, max 0.6 of the width) straddling the boundary at `availableWidth x chatWidth - 12`; immersive system UI.
4. **Landscape full screen with overlay chat** (`fullScreen && fullScreenChatOverlay`): the video takes the screen; chat is drawn over it in an `OverlayChatFrame` (dark theme seeded with the accent, background black at `fullScreenChatOverlayOpacity` default 0.5, vertical placement `overlayChatTop`/`overlayChatHeight` draggable, side switchable), with its own divider limited to the frame's vertical span; the controls area shrinks to the remaining width.
5. **Landscape full screen with hidden chat** (`fullScreen && !fullScreenChatOverlay`): video only; hype train and community highlight become pills at top 56, left/right 12 (`ChannelVideoTopOverlay`).
6. **Tablet landscape beside chat** (large, landscape, not full screen): the chat column gets a 56-px `TabletChatHeader` ("Chat", "Synced to video"/"Delay: N s" pill, "⋯" chat settings) and colour `#0D0D0D` in dark theme, a hairline on the video side; the video column is a `TabletVideoColumn`: 16:9 video at the column width, with the `TabletChannelPanel` under it (title 20 w800 up to 2 lines, chips: category, language, watch streak; Follow 40 + Subscribe; "Also live from your follows" cards 262 px) only while at least 120 px remain; otherwise (or when the picture fills, or when the double tap reached the "plain side column") the video takes the whole column.
7. **Chat only** (`showVideo == false`, from settings or `startChatOnly`): no video; a transparent app bar with a `StreamInfoBar` (compact, no shadows, avatar opens the profile) and a "Videos" action over a blurred gradient; chat scrolls under it.
8. **Offline channel overlay** (stream info null): scrims, back + channel identity "Offline" + similar channels + videos (+ chat overlay toggle in full screen), centre message "Check back soon"/"couldn't load" with "Watch the latest broadcast" or "Past broadcasts" (or "Try again" when the info failed), bottom-right refresh/rotate/fullscreen.
9. **Overlays inside the video** (portrait, side column and tablet; the FULL-SCREEN landscape branch omits the free preview layer and the raid overlay and does not hide the controls under Twitch's web warning — see Known Android defects): the controls overlay (scrims 80-112 px, top row of 44 dp buttons, centre 64-dp play/pause, bottom row LIVE badge + uptime + viewers + mute/side chat/PiP/refresh/rotate/fullscreen), the latency pill (26/28 high, top right), the IVS stats panel (top left under the top row), the content warning cover (opaque), the audience-labels strip (bottom 64), the free preview banner (top left, 44 down) or "preview ended" card, raid departure/arrival overlay, hype/highlight pills, swipe-down hint ("Swipe down for picture-in-picture" over black 40%), captions (bottom 24), audio-only badge, spinners.
10. **Sheets and dialogs**: player settings sheet (quality, volume, captions, measurement, playback: pop-out, auto PiP, background audio; share), Create Clip panel, chatters list (tap on viewers), share sheet, overlay-permission dialog, creator goal dialog, hype train details sheet, followed-channels drawer (width clamp(82% of screen, 260, 360)).
11. **Out-of-screen surfaces**: in-app mini player (the same IVS player and chat handed over), Android picture-in-picture (player only, media pause action), Android floating system-overlay player (pop-out), media notification for background audio, Chromecast.

## Behaviour and state

**Engine resolution and fallback.** `resolvePlayerEngine(setting, isAndroid, forceWeb)`: forceWeb -> web; IVS off Android -> web; else the setting (default `PlayerEngine.ivs`). `_buildPlayerController` constructs `IvsVideoStore` (adopting the mini player's store when restoring the same channel), `NativeVideoStore` or `VideoStore`. Fallback to web happens when: native `ready` throws; IVS `ready` throws and Helix does not confirm the channel offline (`offlineAtStart` false); IVS `onFatalError` (fatal error, exhausted retries, refused token with an explanation, texture creation failure, initial load failure); an adopted IVS player that already failed. Exception: with an active free preview on IVS, an expired preview or a sub-only refusal shows the "preview ended" card instead of swapping. On fallback a refusal notice (sub-only, geoblocked, banned, unauthorized, proxy, wifi required, unsupported device, or usher's English reason on English screens) is shown in a snackbar `channelWebFallback`: EN "{notice} Switching to the web player.", FR "Passage au lecteur web. {notice}", where `{notice}` is `StreamRefusalNotice.text` (e.g. `liveRefusedBecause` "Twitch refused this stream: {reason}" + "." on English screens, or `liveRefused` "Twitch refused this stream."). Without a refusal notice the switch happens silently.

**IVS lifecycle.** `IvsPlaybackStatus` loading -> playing / reconnecting / offline / failed. Startup resolves the usher URL (token from GQL `PlaybackAccessToken_Template`, cached in `PlaybackTokenCache.shared`, possibly preloaded on card press) in parallel with creating the native texture player (`create {config}`), then `load {url}`. Native auto-plays on READY. First PLAYING records `startupMs`. Events: state, qualities (once per load, best first), qualitiesChanged, quality, ad, latency (1 Hz, with bufferMs, rate, lowLatency), rebuffering, stats (only while the panel is open), caption/textTracks, error.

**Recovery policy.** Errors classified (`ERROR_AUTHORIZATION`/401/403 -> fresh token; `ERROR_NOT_AVAILABLE` or 404 on a playlist -> confirm offline with Helix; NOT_SUPPORTED/NO_SOURCE/INVALID_PARAMETER/INVALID_STATE and decode/render INVALID_DATA -> fatal -> web; others -> retry). Retries wait 1 s, 2 s, 4 s; a 4th recoverable error within 60 s falls back. While recovering, further non-fatal errors are the same incident. Offline confirmed -> offline state, offline poll every 60 s, EventSub `stream.online` restarts after 3 s; for 90 s after a live push an offline answer is retried instead of shown. Manual refresh resets everything and forces a fresh token. Token fetch at start retries only network/timeout/5xx errors with the same 1/2/4 s delays.

**Auto latency controller** (IVS only, setting `autoLatency`, default on). Targets: Ultra 1300 ms (floor 700), Standard 2000 ms (floor 1000). Phases: off, settling (6 s calm after a load, 25 s after a stall; calm = no stall, no tick gap > 3 s, a buffer reading), steady, catchingUp (1.05x when the 5-sample upper median latency > target + 400 ms and buffer >= floor + 300 ms, after >= 5 s at 1.0x since the last catch-up; stops when latency <= target + 100 ms after >= 5 s, or buffer <= floor), held (paused/content warning, reconnecting/jumping/not yet playing, buffering, ad 60–180 s, missing buffer or latency reading, lost ticks), stopped (rate refused, rate not kept for 3 ticks, stuck fast after two 1.0x resends, refused 1.0x, or 3 strikes = stalls/unrequested quality down-switches within 15 s of a catch-up, each raising the target 500 ms), draining (stopped after strikes, or a non-LL stream: 1.03x when buffer > floor + 300 and latency > target + 1500 (LL) — non-LL floor 2000 ms, judged on buffer only; stops at target + 1000 or the floor; never within 25 s of a stall, never after a rate doubt, never in an ad). Max rate 1.1x (native clamps 1.0–1.1). A 1-s watchdog ends a fast rate whose ticks stopped. Manual refresh resets strikes and stop (unless the player refused fast rates); automatic reloads keep strikes. PiP and the mini player are not holds.

**Live-edge guard** (all IVS streams): floor = lowest latency since load; threshold = max(5000, floor + 2500) ms; ignores 15 s after a load; drift must last 10 s (immediately after returning to foreground/leaving PiP); cooldown 60 s doubled per relapse; a drift within 2 min of a jump is a relapse; 2 relapses stop it until refresh. A jump fetches a new usher URL (token reused) and reloads (counted in `liveJumps`). Resume after a pause >= 10 s also jumps to live; shorter pauses resume in place.

**Ultra fallback.** With auto latency and Ultra, two network rebuffers (not within 10 s of a viewer/app disturbance: pause, resume, PiP, mini player, content warning, return to foreground) within 60 s make the NEXT streams for 30 minutes open with Standard (memory only; the stats panel row is the label `latencyStatsNextStreams` "Next streams", two spaces, then `latencyBufferFallback` "Standard until {HH:mm} (Ultra stalled)" — FR per the Strings table).

**Chat delay sync** (setting `autoSyncChatDelay`, default on): on open `syncedChatDelay = 0`; IVS: whole seconds of the latency (with 650-ms hysteresis under auto latency), only while playing, reset to 0 when offline/failed; native: whole seconds behind the live edge from position/duration; web: the Twitch stats latency rounded ("5s"), refreshed about once a minute (3 s retries until first reading).

**Overlay visibility.** Visible at start, auto-hides after 5 s; a tap on the video toggles it (a tap ends a pinch first; closes the emote menu or unfocuses the chat input first); showing it refreshes stream info (at most every 5 s for IVS/native; forced for web). Paused or offline -> always shown. Fade 200 ms `Curves.ease`. Long press on the video (web engine with `toggleableOverlay`) swaps ONSET's overlay with Twitch's own controls (medium haptic, overlay shown 3 s).

**Content warning.** `ContentGate.pending` at open (playback continues, PiP/mini player/pop-out forbidden), `covering` when the channel has classification labels and `warnMatureContent` (default on) and it was not accepted this session (player held paused with an explicit pause; web page videos held by script; PiP play action ignored; auto-PiP disarmed), `clear` otherwise. Acceptance is remembered per channel for the app session and shared with VODs; it also lets ONSET click Twitch's own warning in the web page. Labels fetched from Helix `GET /channels`; failure = clear (fail open). Leaving to chat-only is not consent.

**Focus and audio.** `PlaybackFocus`: a VOD/clip opened on top pauses this stream (IVS also suppresses automatic playback) and closing it resumes. IVS audio focus: a call pauses and its end resumes; noisy (headphones unplugged)/permanent loss pauses; media notification play/pause/stop. Background: with "background audio" and "headphones only", 1 s after backgrounding the stream pauses if no headphones are connected (not in PiP, not with auto-PiP armed).

**Preloading.** Pressing a stream card (or an "also live" card: `onTapDown: preloadStream`) calls `PlaybackPreloader.preload(login, engine, authToken)`, which asks GQL for the playback token only when the resolved engine is IVS; the token request is tracked as pending in `PlaybackTokenCache.shared` (capacity 32, LRU, valid until expiry minus 60 s, never persisted), so the player opening the channel reuses it. The preloader's Dio has connect timeout 5 s, receive timeout 8 s, idle timeout 60 s.

**Stream end and live progress.** When the stream goes offline the latest archive VOD is looked up (3 s bound); if it is the broadcast just watched and the screen is current/foreground/not PiP/video shown, a snackbar "The stream ended — playing the broadcast" and the VOD opens. Live position is saved locally every 20 s while playing and to Twitch every 5 min, on pause, on background and on close (GQL viewed-video update, type live).

**Other timers.** Stream info: chat-only web refresh every 60 s; native/IVS refresh on tap (5 s throttle), on app resume in chat-only, on stream pushes (down push retried after 20 s). Web JS soft reset every 10 min. Follow callout after 10 min. Channel info head start 2 s. Hermes points poll 60 s, points refresh coalesced 2 s. Free preview ticker 1 s. Clip polling 5 s up to 60 s.

## Animations and motion

- **Overlay show/hide**: `AnimatedOpacity` 200 ms `Curves.ease` (opacity 1/0) + `IgnorePointer` while hidden (`channel.dart`).
- **PiP swipe-down**: drag translates the video by the distance (0–150 px) and scales it `1 - d/150 x 0.1` (clamped 0.9–1.0); hint overlay `AnimatedOpacity` 150 ms `Curves.easeOut` to black 40% with "Swipe down for picture-in-picture"; release below threshold springs back with an `AnimationController` of 250 ms and `Curves.easeOut` tween; haptics medium on entering the 80-px trigger zone, light on leaving, medium on trigger.
- **Side chat width**: `AnimatedContainer` 200 ms `Curves.ease` (0 ms while the divider is dragged).
- **Chat notification**: `AnimatedSwitcher` 200 ms, in `Curves.easeOut`, out `Curves.easeIn` (default fade).
- **Quality check mark**: `AnimatedOpacity` 160 ms.
- **Info row folding while typing** (`_HiddenWhileTyping`): `TweenAnimationBuilder` height factor + opacity, hide `OnsetMotion.exit` 250 ms with `emphasizedAccelerate`, show `OnsetMotion.enter` 350 ms with `emphasizedDecelerate`; reduce motion -> 150 ms.
- **Free preview banner**: `AnimatedSwitcher` 220 ms, `switchInCurve: Curves.easeOutCubic` (fade in/out).
- **Create Clip panel**: content switch `AnimatedSwitcher` 300 ms (`motionDuration`, 150 ms reduced), `switchInCurve OnsetMotion.standard`, fade + slide from (0, 0.03) (no slide when reduced); live frame opacity 0.28 -> 0.55 via `AnimatedOpacity` 600 ms (150 ms reduced); finished picture `_Reveal` 420 ms `OnsetMotion.standard`: black veil 0.55 -> 0 and scale 0.985 -> 1 (no scale when reduced); indeterminate circular (36 px, stroke 3) and linear (3 px) progress indicators.
- **Press feedback** (`PressScale` on Follow, Subscribe, clip pills/rows, also-live cards, "use the web player"): scale to 0.97 in 100 ms with `OnsetMotion.standard`, release spring `springMomentum` (400 ms, bounce 0.2) from the current value; disabled under reduce motion.
- **Goal progress bar**: `TweenAnimationBuilder` to the new progress, 420 ms `Curves.easeOutCubic`.
- **LIVE badge ping** (tablet): `SignalPing` size 6, ripples to 2.6x, 1200 ms per ripple, 2 ripples per burst then 5 s rest, eased with `OnsetMotion.standard`; paused while the controls are hidden (`TickerMode`), when the app is not resumed or the dot is off-screen; static under reduce motion.
- **Viewer count**: `RollingNumber` 450 ms `OnsetMotion.standard`, digits roll up when increasing and down when decreasing; instant under reduce motion.
- **Spinners**: Material `CircularProgressIndicator` while the texture is missing or the IVS status is loading/reconnecting.
- **Raid departure delay**: 1350 ms (700 ms under reduce motion) before switching; arrival fade 500 ms (overlay widget is another leaf).
- **Latency pill**: no animation; `RepaintBoundary` so its 1-Hz change never repaints the video.
- Reduce motion is read through `reduceMotionOf(context)` / `motionDuration`; everything listed as reduced above becomes a 150-ms fade or instant.

## Network and data

- **Playback token (GQL)**: `POST https://gql.twitch.tv/gql`, header `Client-ID: kimne78kx3ncx6brgo4mv6wki5h1ko` (+ `Authorization: OAuth <twitch.tv web token>` when available; the app's own login token is never sent because GQL refuses it), body `{operationName: 'PlaybackAccessToken_Template', query, variables: {isLive: true, login, isVod: false, vodID: '', playerType: 'embed'}}`. Query text: `query PlaybackAccessToken_Template($login: String!, $isLive: Boolean!, $vodID: ID!, $isVod: Boolean!, $playerType: String!) {  streamPlaybackAccessToken(channelName: $login, params: {platform: "web", playerBackend: "mediaplayer", playerType: $playerType}) @include(if: $isLive) {    value signature authorization { forbiddenReasonCode } __typename  }  videoPlaybackAccessToken(id: $vodID, params: {platform: "web", playerBackend: "mediaplayer", playerType: $playerType}) @include(if: $isVod) {    value signature __typename  }}`; if the response has `errors` and no token, it is retried with the plain query (without `authorization { forbiddenReasonCode }`). Missing token -> "No playback access token (channel offline?)". The token's `forbiddenReasonCode` becomes a `PlaybackRefusal`; refused tokens are not cached. An authenticated 401 renews the web token in the background and falls back to anonymous at once. Cache keys: login (anonymous) or `login#<hash of auth token>`.

## PC equivalent

Windows first (Rust, Slint UI, libmpv), macOS/Linux later. Nothing is dropped; every Android-only mechanism gets a concrete replacement:
- **Engines**: (1) primary = libmpv rendered into the Slint window through `mpv_render_context` (OpenGL or D3D11 interop), fed by a custom low-latency HLS fetcher: it requests the usher master playlist with exactly the parameters above, picks the variant, polls the media playlist, reads `#EXT-X-TWITCH-PREFETCH:<uri>` lines (the LL "prefetch" parts enabled by `fast_bread=true`) and downloads the next segments before they are listed as complete, pushing bytes into mpv through a custom stream protocol (`mpv_stream_cb_add_ro`) so the demuxer always sits at the newest segment; it exposes `latencyMs` (wall clock minus program-date-time of the frame on screen + buffered), `bufferMs` (queued segments + `demuxer-cache-duration`), `lowLatency` (prefetch tags present), `rate` (mpv `speed`), ad cues (`#EXT-X-DATERANGE` with `CLASS="twitch-stitched-ad"`/`X-TV-TWITCH-AD-*`, duration) and ticks once per second — the same event map as `IvsPlayerCore` so `IvsVideoStore`'s logic (recovery, live-edge guard, latency controller, Ultra fallback, quality restore, captions) ports unchanged. ### Known Android defects

Real bugs of the Android code, recorded so the port decides deliberately (fix by default on PC):
1. **Full-screen landscape lacks the free-preview and raid layers** (`channel.dart`, full-screen branch): it builds `gated(Stack[player, chrome/overlay chat, ChannelVideoTopOverlay])` instead of the `video` stack, so in full screen there is no free-preview banner, no "preview ended" card, no "use the web player" button, and no "Heading to" departure/arrival overlay — while the preview timer, its pause on expiry, and the raid departure timer/switch still run. PC fix: one video-layer composition used in every layout (including fullscreen) containing FreePreviewLayer and RaidOverlayLayer.
2. **Controls over Twitch's web warning in full screen**: the full-screen branch draws `chrome` when `showOverlay` is true without `!_webGateUp`, so ONSET's controls stay over Twitch's own content-classification interstitial (its buttons may be unreachable). PC fix: apply `show_overlay && !web_gate_visible` everywhere.
3. **`_createTexture` silent spinner**: when the native `create` returns null, the store attaches no channel, never calls `_fail()`, and `_textureReady` completes true; `load` is sent to a null channel and the screen shows the spinner forever (no fallback). PC fix: a failed surface/player creation = `fail()` -> web fallback.
4. **One shared rate-trouble flag**: `_rateTroubleReported` is shared by `_onRateFailed` and the stuck-fast report, so at most one telemetry record per stream across both failure kinds. PC fix: one flag per kind (behaviour of the controller unchanged).
5. **`clock.now()` vs injected `_now`**: `_wentLiveAt`/`_justWentLive` (90 s trust window) use the global clock while the rest of the store uses the injected `_now`. PC fix: use the store's injected clock everywhere.
6. **Native store empties the quality menu on no variants**: `_load()` assigns `availableStreamQualities` (empty) before throwing `StateError` when no variants come back, so a refresh that finds none leaves an empty menu while the previous variant may still play. PC fix: assign only after the non-empty check.
7. **Clip poll error at 60 s**: in `CreateClipStore._watch` `waited` grows on failed looks too; a failed `getCreatedClip` at or after the 60 s deadline ends in `failed(unknown)` although the clip may exist. PC fix: on the final tick, retry once (or treat a network error as "unknown, offer Retry/Open on twitch.tv") instead of reporting failure.
8. **Creator goals never shown over the video**: goals go only to `ChannelChatTopOverlay`, which renders nothing for placement `pill` (landscape full screen, chat hidden) or `none` (landscape phone with the keyboard up), and `ChannelVideoTopOverlay` has no goals: goals vanish in those cases. PC fix: show goals as a pill/row with the video pills when the chat is hidden.
9. **A failed watch-streak read wipes the shown streak**: `getWatchStreak` returning null sets `watchStreak = null` (not just the failure flag), hiding a known streak and its share offer after a transient error, silently. PC fix: keep the last known streak on failure; only replace it on a successful read.
10. **Celebrations without an event id are never de-duplicated**: a replayed/duplicated payload lacking `eventId` plays again every time. PC fix: de-duplicate those by a content hash (effect, intensity, user, timestamp) within the same 50-entry window.
11. **Pinch not re-armed**: `PinchToFill._up` resets the start distance when back to two fingers but not `_decided`, so a second pinch in the same gesture (or after a 3->2 transition) never fires. PC fix: reset `decided` whenever exactly two pointers remain.
12. **FreePreviewController clock and synchronous expiry**: defaults to `DateTime.now` (not the injectable `clock.now`) and its constructor can call `expire()`/`onExpired` synchronously when `endsAt <= now`. PC fix: use the app clock and defer the first expiry to the next event-loop turn.
13. **`refreshFollowing` ignores the 2 s head start and logs the full error**: the post-twitch.tv refresh reads immediately even during the head start, and logs `$error` (may contain request details). PC fix: queue it until the head start ends; log the error type only.

## Source files

### lib/screens/channel/video/player_engine_resolver.dart

Single pure function `resolvePlayerEngine({required PlayerEngine setting, required bool isAndroid, required bool forceWeb}) -> PlayerEngine`. Decision table, evaluated in this exact order:
1. `forceWeb == true` (set after a runtime fallback, see `ChannelState`/`VideoStore` engine fallback and IVS `allEnginesFailed`) -> `PlayerEngine.twitchWeb` regardless of the setting.
2. `setting == PlayerEngine.ivs && !isAndroid` -> `PlayerEngine.twitchWeb` (the Amazon IVS engine only has a native Android implementation; iOS must never "spin forever" waiting for a native view that does not exist).
3. Otherwise -> the setting unchanged (`ivs` on Android, `nativeLowLatency` on any platform, `twitchWeb` everywhere).
Note `nativeLowLatency` (media_kit/mpv) is NOT remapped on iOS: it has a cross-platform implementation. The three engines are the `PlayerEngine` enum of `SettingsStore` (`ivs` = default, Amazon IVS; `nativeLowLatency` = media_kit HLS fallback; `twitchWeb` = original Twitch web embed in a WebView). Unknown saved enum values deserialize to `PlayerEngine.ivs` (`unknownEnumValue`).
Tested by: `test/screens/channel/video/player_engine_resolver_test.dart` — the exact assertions are the test titles listed for these files under "Tested behaviour" (the behavioural oracle).
PC: the Rust app has one primary engine (libmpv + custom low-latency HLS fetcher) that plays the role of `ivs`; `nativeLowLatency` maps to "plain libmpv HLS without the custom fetcher", and `twitchWeb` maps to a WebView2 hosting `player.twitch.tv`. Keep the same resolver signature with `is_supported(engine)` in place of `isAndroid` and the same `force_web` latch.

### lib/screens/channel/video/live_state_watcher.dart

Pushes a channel GOING LIVE through EventSub so an offline player restarts without waiting for a poll.
- `enum LiveChange { online, maybeMissed }` — `maybeMissed` = "listening again after a gap: a change may have been missed".
- `_numericId = RegExp(r'^[0-9]{1,20}$')`; the constructor throws `ArgumentError.value(broadcasterId, 'broadcasterId')` if the id is not 1-20 ASCII digits (prevents injecting into the subscription condition).
- Constructor `LiveStateWatcher({required EventSubHandle Function(EventSubTopic) listen, required String broadcasterId, required void Function(LiveChange) onChange})` subscribes to `EventSubTopic('stream.online', '1', {'broadcaster_user_id': broadcasterId})` (EventSub `stream.online` v1 needs a logged-in viewer but no scope). It listens to `handle.events`: `EventSubData()` -> `onChange(LiveChange.online)`; `EventSubListening(afterGap: true)` -> `onChange(LiveChange.maybeMissed)`; anything else ignored.
- Going OFFLINE is deliberately not subscribed: the player sees it first, and each topic costs from a budget of 10 subscriptions per EventSub connection.
- `dispose()` cancels the handle and (unawaited) the stream subscription.
Used by the channel screen: on `online`/`maybeMissed` while the stream is offline the player refreshes / re-reads the stream (see `channel.dart`). Tested by `test/screens/channel/video/live_state_watcher_test.dart` (rejects non-numeric ids, subscribes to stream.online v1 with the broadcaster condition, maps data -> online, listening-after-gap -> maybeMissed, plain listening ignored, dispose cancels).
PC: same object over the Rust EventSub websocket client (`wss://eventsub.wss.twitch.tv/ws`), subscription created through Helix `POST /eventsub/subscriptions` with the user token.

### lib/screens/channel/video/buffer_fallback.dart

`BufferFallback` is the safety net of the Ultra buffer profile when auto latency is on. Constructor `BufferFallback({DateTime Function()? now})` (defaults to `clock.now`, testable). `static final shared = BufferFallback()` is the single instance all live players share (in-memory only: an app restart tries Ultra again; the saved setting is never changed).
Constants: `stalls = 2` rebuffers, `window = Duration(seconds: 60)`, `holdFor = Duration(minutes: 30)` ("long enough for a bad network spell, short enough that a good one gets Ultra back the same evening").
State: `_recent` list of rebuffer timestamps, `_until` nullable DateTime.
- `until` getter: null when `_until` is null or `now >= _until`; otherwise `_until`.
- `active` = `until != null`.
- `onUltraRebuffer() -> bool`: removes timestamps older than `window` (strictly `now - at > 60 s`), appends now; if fewer than 2 remain returns false; else clears the list, sets `_until = now + 30 min`, returns true ("this one tripped it").
It NEVER touches the stream that stalled — the buffer profile is fixed when the player is created and changing it would reload the picture. Only streams opened during the hold use Standard (`IvsPlayerConfig.fromSettings`). Tested by `test/screens/channel/video/buffer_fallback_test.dart` (one stall does not trip; two within 60 s trip and return true; two 61 s apart do not; hold ends after 30 min; trip clears the window so the next stall alone does not re-trip).
PC: identical struct, process-global `static` behind a mutex; consulted when building the fetcher's buffer config.

### lib/screens/channel/video/web_page_init.dart

`WebPageInit` decides when the live WEB player page gets set up (`initVideo`). Fields: `isPlayerPage(String url) -> bool`, `ensureLowLatency() -> Future<bool>` (turns the Twitch player's own low-latency mode on; true when the page reloads itself to apply it), `init() -> Future<void>` (sets the loaded page up), optional `everyPage()` (runs on every page start and finish, whatever the page and even for reloads the page does on its own, BEFORE setup — "what each new document must be told again at once", e.g. the theater/zoom CSS and the gate poller). `needsInit = true` (set back to true by a refresh which loads the page anew). `_lowLatencyChecked = false`.
- `onPageStarted(url)` -> `everyPage?.call()` only.
- `onPageFinished(url)`: calls `everyPage`; returns if `!isPlayerPage(url) || !needsInit`; sets `needsInit = false` BEFORE awaiting anything (so a second page-finished event meanwhile never sets the page up twice); if the low-latency check has not run, marks it run (once per player — a page that would not keep the setting must not reload forever) and `if (await ensureLowLatency())` sets `needsInit = true` and returns (the reloaded page will be set up instead); otherwise `await init()`.
Tested by: `test/screens/channel/video/web_page_init_test.dart` — the exact assertions are the test titles listed for these files under "Tested behaviour" (the behavioural oracle).
PC: the WebView2 fallback uses the same state machine on `NavigationStarting`/`NavigationCompleted`.

### lib/screens/channel/video/latency_stats.dart

Formatting and statistics helpers.
- `formatLatency(int ms) -> String`: integer tenths `tenths = (ms.clamp(0, 1<<31) / 100).round()`; output `'${tenths ~/ 10}.${tenths % 10}s'` (e.g. 2345 -> "2.3s", 2350 -> "2.4s" (round half away), negative -> "0.0s"). Never depends on float quirks.
- `displayLatency(String latency, AppLocalizations l)`: if the string matches `^(\d+(?:\.\d+)?)s$` returns `l.latencySeconds(value: localDecimal(match))` (EN "2.3s", FR "2,3 s"); any other text (placeholder "—", reconnect "↻ 2/5") is returned unchanged.
- `chatDelaySecondsFromLatency(int ms) = (ms / 1000).clamp(0, 60).round()` — whole seconds only so the delayed chat queue is not rebuilt each tick.
- `chatDelaySecondsWithHysteresis(int ms, int? current, {int marginMs = 150})`: rounded = above; if current null or equal returns rounded; else returns rounded only if `|ms - current*1000| >= 500 + marginMs` (i.e. 650 ms away from current), else keeps current. Rationale: auto latency holds near a target that can sit near a half second (Ultra aims 1.3 s, wanders to 1.7 s).
- `formatBitrate(int? bps, l)`: null/<=0 -> "—"; >= 1,000,000 -> `l.latencyMegabits(value: localDecimal((bps/1e6).toStringAsFixed(1)))` (EN "4.2 Mbps", FR "4,2 Mbit/s"); else `l.latencyKilobits(value: (bps/1000).ceil())`.
- `LatencySampler({capacity = 300})` (five minutes at 1 Hz): `Queue<int>`; `add(ms)` appends and drops oldest beyond capacity; `reset()`; `count`; `min`, `max`; `median` = upper median `sorted[len ~/ 2]`; `p90` nearest rank `sorted[ceil(0.9*len) - 1]`; all null when empty.

### lib/screens/channel/video/pinch_to_fill.dart

- `kPinchThreshold = 1.15`.
- `bool? pinchVerdict(double startDistance, double distance)`: null if start <= 0; ratio = distance/start; `>= 1.15` -> true (fill/zoom to cover); `<= 1/1.15` (~0.8696) -> false (fit); else null (too small to tell).
- `PinchToFill({required ValueChanged<bool> onPinch, required ValueNotifier<bool> pinching, required Widget child})` reads RAW pointers through a `Listener` (onPointerDown/Move/Up/Cancel), so it never wins the gesture arena against taps/drags; the others read `pinching` to stand down (no swipe-to-PiP, no tap) while two fingers are down.
- State: `_pointers` map id->position, `_startDistance`, `_decided`.
- Down: 1 pointer -> new gesture, `pinching.value = false`; 2 -> `pinching.value = true`, start distance = current distance of the first two pointers, `_decided = false`; 3+ -> `_startDistance = null` (not a pinch until back to two).
- Move: update position; if exactly 2 pointers, start known and not decided, compute verdict; first non-null verdict -> `_decided = true; onPinch(fill)` (ONCE per pinch).
- Up/cancel: remove pointer; `_startDistance = pointers.length == 2 ? currentDistance : null` (back to two fingers -> measure from where they are now). `_decided` is NOT reset here (only in `_down` when the count reaches exactly 2), so after a 3->2 transition, or once the pinch already fired, no second verdict fires within the same gesture (see Known Android defects). `pinching` stays true until the next gesture starts with one finger, so the tap or swipe that ends a pinch is still read as a pinch.

### lib/screens/channel/video/live_edge_guard.dart

`LiveEdgeGuard({DateTime Function()? now})` (default `clock.now`) decides when the native live player has drifted so far behind live that it should JUMP back (by reloading the stream, which starts at the edge). It is a safety net on top of the speed-up catch-up, not a replacement: the IVS SDK's low-latency buffer control has `rebufferPenaltySeconds` 1 s and `minBufferCeilingSeconds` 6 s (IVS config defaults) so the target can grow after stalls, and a network drop the player resumes from leaves it behind by as long as it lasted — chat (sent ~2 s behind live) then runs ahead of the picture.
Constants:
- `minThresholdMs = 5000` — never jump below 5 s (healthy LL stream reads ~2-3.5 s on the native player: usher's newest full segment is 0.3-2.8 s old, prefetch parts complete 0.0-0.9 s after their end, plus IVS 1 s LL buffer).
- `marginMs = 2500` above this load's floor (two rebuffer penalties and a half).
- `settle = 15 s` — samples right after a load only measure the floor.
- `sustain = 10 s` — the drift has to last.
- `maxSampleGap = 3 s` — a longer gap means the ticker stopped (background, player recreated): drift has to be seen again.
- `cooldown = 60 s` minimum between jumps, doubled per relapse in a row (`cooldown * (1 << relapses)`).
- `relapseWindow = 2 min` — a drift starting this soon after a jump means it did not hold.
- `maxRelapses = 2` — then the guard stops for the session (until a manual refresh -> `reset`).

### lib/screens/channel/video/ivs_recovery.dart

How the IVS engine reacts to native player errors.
`enum IvsErrorKind { recoverable, refreshToken, offline, fatal }` — recoverable (network, timeout, unknown: reload with a new usher URL), refreshToken (playback token rejected: reload with a freshly fetched token), offline (stream gone: show offline state), fatal (device cannot play: use the WebView engine).
`classifyIvsError({String? errorType, int? code, String? source})` (errorType is the IVS `ErrorType` enum name, code HTTP-ish, source e.g. `MasterPlaylist`, `Segment`, `Decode`; src lowercased), in order:
1. `errorType == 'ERROR_AUTHORIZATION' || code == 401 || code == 403` -> refreshToken.
2. `errorType == 'ERROR_NOT_AVAILABLE' || (code == 404 && src contains 'playlist')` -> offline.
3. `ERROR_NOT_SUPPORTED`, `ERROR_NO_SOURCE`, `ERROR_INVALID_PARAMETER`, `ERROR_INVALID_STATE` -> fatal.
4. `ERROR_INVALID_DATA` -> fatal if src contains `decode` or `render`, else recoverable.
5. anything else -> recoverable (the retry budget still bounds it).
`sealed class IvsRecoveryAction` with `retry(Duration delay, {bool freshToken})` = `IvsRetry` (value equality on delay+freshToken, `toString` `IvsRetry($delay, freshToken: $freshToken)`), `offline()` = `IvsOffline`, `fallback()` = `IvsFallback` (value-equal singletons).
`IvsRecoveryPolicy({backoff = [1 s, 2 s, 4 s], window = 60 s, now})` — bounded: each retry waits the next backoff; once every delay was used inside the rolling window the player falls back to the WebView engine, so a flapping stream always ends in a fallback rather than looping. `maxAttempts = backoff.length` (3); `attemptsInWindow` (prunes first). `onError(kind)`: offline -> `IvsOffline`; fatal -> `IvsFallback`; recoverable/refreshToken -> prune attempts older than 60 s; if attempts >= 3 -> fallback; else delay = backoff[attempts], record now, return `IvsRetry(delay, freshToken: kind == refreshToken)`. `tokenRetryDelay(int attempt)` -> backoff[attempt] or null when exhausted (for the token fetch). `reset()` clears attempts.
`isRetryableTokenError(Object error)`: `NetworkException` or app `TimeoutException` -> true; `ServerException` with statusCode >= 500 -> true; `dart:async TimeoutException` -> true; else false (missing token = channel offline, client errors are not retried).

### lib/screens/channel/video/latency_controller.dart

The AUTO LATENCY controller: keeps a live stream close to live WITHOUT ever reloading it, by playing very slightly faster while it sits behind its target. Pure Dart, injectable clock (`clock.now` default); the store feeds it native ticks and applies `rate`.

Why a rate and not the SDK: the IVS speed-up catch-up only drains buffer above the LL buffer target, at 1.03x, and that target grows after stalls (1 s rebuffer penalty, up to the ceiling) and never shrinks back — a stream that settled 2.2 s behind after a network burst stays there. Playing at `catchUpRate` drains it over a few seconds, invisibly (picture never stops; audio keeps pitch — the SDK applies the rate through `PlaybackParams` speed). With auto latency on, the player is created WITHOUT the SDK speed-up (`IvsPlayerConfig.toCreateArgs`), and where the catch-up cannot run (stopped after stalls, stream without low latency) a timid DRAIN takes the SDK's place.

**Enums**
- `AutoLatencyPhase`: `off` (setting off for this stream; player left as it was), `stopped` (gave up for this stream until a manual refresh: rate refused/not kept, or catching up kept causing stalls), `held` (not allowed now: paused, ad, reconnecting, no buffer/latency reading, not LL...), `draining` (stopped or non-LL stream: drains a clearly excessive buffer at `drainRate`), `settling` (waiting for the connection to be calm long enough), `steady` (close enough: 1.0x), `catchingUp` (playing at `catchUpRate`).
- `AutoLatencyReason`: `ad`, `noBufferReading`, `noLatencyReading`, `noLatencyTicks` (ticks stopped during a catch-up), `notLowLatency`, `paused` (viewer paused or a page covers the player), `reconnecting` (or jumps to live, or not playing yet), `buffering`, `rateRefused`, `rateNotKept`, `stallsAfterCatchUp`, `stuckFast` (player stayed fast after 1.0x asked; `reasonRate` says how fast), `notBackToNormal` (player refused to return to 1.0x).
- `AutoLatencyStatus({required phase, required double rate, int? targetMs, reason, double? reasonRate, int? calmLeftSeconds})` — value equality over all six fields so the store notifies only when something shown changed.

**Construction**
- `LatencyController({required bool enabled, required int targetMs, required int bufferFloorMs, now})`; initial phase `settling` if enabled else `off`.
- `LatencyController.forProfile(BufferProfile, {required enabled, now})`: Ultra -> target 1300 ms, floor 700 ms (Ultra measured holding 1.1-1.2 s on a calm connection; SDK min buffer 0.5 s, floor keeps 0.2 s above it); Standard -> target 2000 ms, floor 1000 ms (lands at 1.8-2.0 s after a refresh; SDK min buffer 1.0 s).

**Constants (exact)**
`catchUpRate = 1.05` (5% is invisible/inaudible with pitch kept; 1.7x the SDK's 1.03x drain speed: 0.8 s drift goes in 16 s); `maxLiveRate = 1.1` (native live player's max, it clamps too); `startAboveMs = 400`; `stopAboveMs = 100`; `bufferHeadroomMs = 300`; `startSettle = 6 s`; `calmAfterStall = 25 s`; `maxSampleGap = 3 s`; `restCooldown = 5 s`; `minCatchUp = 5 s` (rate changes at most every 5 s; overshoot costs at most 0.25 s of buffer); `strikeWindow = 15 s`; `strikeBackoffMs = 500`; `maxStrikes = 3`; `smoothing = 5` (median window); `rateNotHeldTicks = 3`; `drainRate = 1.03`; `drainAboveMs = 1500`; `drainStopAboveMs = 1000`; `normalLatencyFloorMs = 2000` (IVS normal-latency min buffer); `minAdHold = 60 s`; `maxAdHold = 180 s`.

**State**: `_latencies`, `_buffers` (windows of 5), `_lastSampleAt`, `_calmSince`, `_requiredCalm` (= startSettle initially), `_lastCatchUpEnd`, `_catchUpStart`, `_adUntil`, `_strikes`, `_notHeld`, `_stopped`, `_stopReason`, `_stopRate`, `_drainable`, `_lastStallAt`, `_rate = 1.0`, `_phase`, `_reason`, `_reasonRate`, `_calmLeftSeconds`.
Getters: `rate`; `effectiveTargetMs = targetMs + strikes*500`; `status` (targetMs only when enabled; calmLeftSeconds only while settling).

**Methods**
- `onLoad()`: to normal rate; lastCatchUpEnd null; adUntil null; restart calm with `startSettle` (6 s); if active (enabled && !stopped) phase settling; if stopped re-set the stopped phase. Strikes are KEPT (an automatic reload does not fix the connection).
- `reset()` (manual refresh): strikes 0, stopped false, stopReason null, drainable false, notHeld 0; if enabled phase settling.
- `onStall()`: if disabled return; `_lastStallAt = now`; if stopped: normal rate, clear calm, stopped phase, return. Else `_strikeIfRecent()` (if a catch-up runs or ended within 15 s: strikes++; at 3 -> `_stop(stallsAfterCatchUp, drainable: true)`), normal rate, restart calm with `calmAfterStall` 25 s, settling if still active.
- `onQualityChanged()` (quality went DOWN without being asked): only if active and a recent catch-up -> treated as `onStall()` (ABR down-switch on a drained buffer).
- `onAd(Duration duration)`: if enabled, `_adUntil = now + clamp(duration, 60 s, 180 s)`, hold with reason `ad`.
- `hold(reason)`: if enabled, hold, and `_lastCatchUpEnd = null` (the viewer ended it: a stall after resuming is not a strike).
- `onRateRejected()` = `stop(rateRefused)`.
- `stop(reason, {rate})` = `_stop(reason, drainable: false, rate)`.
- `_stop`: if disabled return; normal rate; `_drainable = drainable && (!_stopped || _drainable)` (a doubt about the rate, once seen, keeps the drain off for the stream); stopped = true; reason/rate saved; clear calm; stopped phase.
- `onMissingLatency()`: NO `enabled` check (unlike `hold`/`onAd`/`onStall`): `_lastSampleAt = now`, then `_holdFor(noLatencyReading)` = `_toNormalRate()`, `_clearCalm()`, phase stopped (if stopped) else held(noLatencyReading) — even when disabled this sets phase `held` with targetMs null; the IVS store only calls it with auto latency on.
- `checkTicks() -> bool` (watchdog between ticks): returns false if rate <= 1, if there is no sample yet (`_lastSampleAt == null`), or if `now - lastSample <= 3 s` (strict: exactly 3 s is not a gap); otherwise `_holdFor(noLatencyTicks)` and returns true.
- `onSample({required int latencyMs, int? bufferMs, bool? lowLatency, double? nativeRate, AutoLatencyReason? hold}) -> double`:
  1. record sample time; if disabled return rate.
  2. gap > 3 s since previous sample -> `_clearCalm()` (back to 1.0x, forget calm and windows).
  3. held reason = explicit `hold` ?? (`ad` while now < adUntil) ?? (`noBufferReading` if bufferMs null) -> `_holdFor(reason)` (normal rate, clear calm, phase held(reason) or stopped), return.
  4. stopped: if not drainable return rate; else `_drain(latency, buffer, nativeRate, lowLatency: lowLatency != false)`.
  5. `lowLatency == false` -> `_drain(..., lowLatency: false)`.
  6. push latency and buffer into windows; `calmSince ??= now`; if calm < required calm -> normal rate, `calmLeftSeconds = (required - calm).inSeconds`, phase settling, return.
  7. latency = upper median of window; buffer = median; target = effective target.
  8. If currently fast (rate > 1): if `_rateNotKept(nativeRate)` return; `ran = now - catchUpStart >= 5 s`; if `(ran && latency <= target + 100) || buffer <= floor` -> normal rate, phase steady. Return.
  9. Else: `rested = lastCatchUpEnd == null || now - lastCatchUpEnd >= 5 s`; if rested && latency > target + 400 && buffer >= floor + 300 -> rate = 1.05, catchUpStart = now, notHeld 0, phase catchingUp; else phase steady. Return rate.
- `_drain(latency, buffer, nativeRate, {lowLatency})`: push both; `rest()` = normal rate and (stopped ? stopped phase : held(notLowLatency)). `calm = lastStall == null || now - lastStall >= 25 s`; if not calm or fewer than 5 samples -> rest. floor = lowLatency ? bufferFloorMs : 2000. If fast: rateNotKept -> return; `buffer <= floor || (lowLatency && latency <= target + 1000)` -> rest. Else if `buffer > floor + 300 && (!lowLatency || latency > target + 1500)` -> rate 1.03, notHeld 0, phase draining(reason: stopped ? stopReason : notLowLatency, reasonRate: stopped ? stopRate : null); else rest. (Never during an ad: the hold short-circuits first — like the SDK's `MediaPlayer::checkBufferSpeedUp` that skips while its ad flag is set.)
- `_rateNotKept(nativeRate)`: null or within 0.005 of the asked rate -> notHeld = 0, false; else `++notHeld < 3` -> false; else `stop(rateNotKept)`, true.
- `_recentCatchUp`: catching up (rate == 1.05) or last catch-up end within 15 s. The drain is never a catch-up (its stalls are never strikes).
- `_toNormalRate()`: if catching up records `_lastCatchUpEnd = now`; rate 1.0; notHeld 0. `_restartCalm(required)`; `_clearCalm()` (normal rate, calmSince null, windows cleared). `_median` = upper median.
PiP and the mini player are NOT holds: the rate is invisible there too and every check keeps running.

**Text helpers**
- `describeAutoLatency(AutoLatencyStatus? status, l)`: null -> `latencyAutoOff`. `aimed(text)` = `latencyAutoWithTarget(status: text, latency: displayLatency(formatLatency(targetMs)))` when targetMs non-null. Per phase: off -> `latencyAutoOff`; stopped -> `latencyAutoStopped(reason: reason == null ? latencyReasonGaveUp : reasonText)`; held -> aimed(`latencyAutoHeld(reason: reason == null ? latencyReasonHeld : reasonText)`); settling -> aimed(calmLeftSeconds != null ? `latencyAutoSettlingSeconds(seconds)` : `latencyAutoSettling`); steady -> aimed(`latencyAutoSteady`); catchingUp -> aimed(`latencyAutoCatchingUp(rate: "1.05")`); draining -> reason null ? `latencyAutoDraining(rate)` : `latencyAutoDrainingBecause(rate, reason)` (draining is never "aimed").
- `_rateText(rate) = localDecimal(rate.toStringAsFixed(2))` ("1.05" / "1,05").
- `autoLatencyReasonText(reason, l, {rate})`: null -> ''; each reason -> its `latencyReason*` key; `stuckFast` -> `latencyReasonStuckFast(rate: _rateText(rate ?? 1))`; `notBackToNormal` -> `latencyReasonNotBackToNormal(rate: localDecimal('1.0'))`.
- `describeBufferFallback(DateTime? until, l)`: null -> null; else `latencyBufferFallback(time: 'HH:mm')` (local wall clock of `until`, zero-padded).

PC: port 1:1 into Rust; apply `rate` via mpv `speed` (with `audio-pitch-correction=yes`, the default `scaletempo2`); `nativeRate` read back from mpv `speed`; `bufferMs` from `demuxer-cache-duration` (plus the fetcher's queued prefetch); `lowLatency` = the playlist exposes `#EXT-X-TWITCH-PREFETCH`; ad hold driven by `#EXT-X-DATERANGE` ad cues (`CLASS="twitch-stitched-ad"`, `DURATION`).

### lib/screens/channel/video/stores/ivs_video_store.dart

`IvsVideoStore` (MobX) drives the native Amazon IVS player (Twitch's own video technology) over MethodChannels — the DEFAULT engine (`PlayerEngine.ivs`, Android only). Implements `PlayerController`, `StreamInfoStatus`, `StreamPushTarget`. This is the heart of the low-latency mission.

**Enum** `IvsPlaybackStatus { loading, playing, reconnecting, offline, failed }` (drives the spinner, the "↻ n/3" reconnect label, the offline UI and the fallback decision).

**Constructor** `IvsVideoStoreBase({required userLogin, required userId, required twitchApi, required authStore, required settingsStore, TwitchStreamService? streamService, @visibleForTesting IvsRecoveryPolicy? recoveryPolicy, EventSubHandle Function(EventSubTopic)? listenEvents, liveStartDelay = 3 s, offlinePollInterval = 60 s, autoLatencyWatchdog = 1 s, backgroundPauseDelay = 1 s, BackgroundAudio? backgroundAudio, AudioFocus? audioFocus, DateTime Function()? now, BufferFallback? bufferFallback, Future<bool?> Function()? isMetered})`:

**Startup**
- `_resolveUrl()` (@action): loop `attempt = 0..`: `_url = await getUsherUrl(userLogin, authToken: authStore.twitchWebToken)` (only the twitch.tv web token is sent: GQL refuses the app's login token; the service falls back to anonymous when refused); on error, `delay = _recovery.tokenRetryDelay(attempt)` (1, 2, 4 s, then null); rethrow if disposed, no delay left, or `!isRetryableTokenError(e)` (a missing token = offline throws at once); else wait and retry.
- `_createTexture()`: returns at once if disposed or `_textureId` is already set; `onset/ivs-texture-manager.create({'config': playerConfig.toCreateArgs()})` -> texture id; a null id returns SILENTLY (no channel attached, no `_fail()`, `_textureReady` still completes true, so `load` goes to a null channel: the spinner stays — see Known Android defects); if disposed meanwhile -> `dispose({'textureId'})` at once (else audio with no owner); else `attachTextureChannel(id)` (per-texture channel `onset/ivs-texture/<id>`, handler forwards `onEvent` maps to `_onEvent`), `textureId = id`, and resend stats-enabled if the panel is already open.
- `_loadInitial(epoch)`: skipped if a refresh/dispose bumped the epoch or the URL is null; `_loadSource` errors -> `_fail()`.
- `_loadSource(url, epoch)`: awaits `_textureReady` (false -> `_fail()`); stale epoch -> return; `_hasPlayed = false`; `_lastLatencySecond = null`; `_liveEdge.onLoad()`; with auto latency: resend counters reset, `_playingQuality = null`, `_autoLatency.onLoad()`, `_applyAutoRate()`; `invokeMethod('load', {'url'})`.
- `_settleFailedStart(error)`: unless disposed or the error is retryable, checks Helix `getStream` — a 404 `ApiException` means offline; other unexpected errors -> `_reportUnexpected('offline check at start')` (debugPrint + `Telemetry.recordError(reason: 'ivs offline check at start')`). Sets status offline (streamInfo null, paused true, then force stream-info update) or failed. Completes `offlineAtStart` with `offline && !disposed` — the channel screen keeps the native player for a confirmed offline channel (it restarts when live) and falls back to the WebView for anything else (maybe a Twitch change).

**Going live again**
- `_trustLivePush = 90 s`: for 90 s after an EventSub "went live", an offline answer from Helix (which lags) or an unready playlist is retried, not shown (`_justWentLive`). Note: `_wentLiveAt` and `_justWentLive` use the global `clock.now()`, not the injected `_now` (the 90 s window follows the global clock in tests).
- `_onLiveChange(online)`: `_wentLiveAt = now`; (re)start `_liveStartTimer = Timer(liveStartDelay 3 s, () => if still offline handleRefresh())`. `maybeMissed` -> if offline `_restartIfLive()`.
- `_restartIfLive()`: `getStream`; 404 -> still offline (other ApiExceptions logged), unexpected -> reported; success and still offline -> `handleRefresh()`.
- `applyStreamPush(StreamUpPush)` -> `_onLiveChange(online)` (other pushes as in the other engines: value pushes patch streamInfo; `StreamDownPush` -> force update + 20 s retry).

**Native events** (`_onEvent`, @action) — protocol from `IvsPlayerCore.kt`:
- `state` {state: IDLE|READY|BUFFERING|PLAYING|ENDED}: `_isPlaying = state == PLAYING`; PLAYING -> `_hasPlayed`, `_pausedAt = null`, `_lastErrorRefused = false`, `_audioNotificationStopped = false`; if `_holdsPlayback` (a VOD on top has focus, or covered) -> `pause` (and mark held for the gate when covered); `_paused = false`; status playing; first time: stop stopwatch, `startupMs`. IDLE/ENDED -> paused true, `_pausedAt ??= now`. Android `pip.setIsPlaying(!paused)`. (Native auto-plays on READY for live.)
- `qualities` {list} (once per load, sent on first PLAYING, sorted best first by height, framerate, bitrate): `availableStreamQualities = ['Auto', ...list]`; `_restoreQuality(list)`; `_resendSound()`; `captions.onLoad()`.
- `caption` / `textTracks` -> `captions.handleEvent(e)`.
- `qualitiesChanged` {list} (ladder grew/shrank while playing — Twitch negotiates 1440p HEVC/AV1 later): rebuild list keeping the current pick's entry (or index 0); if no session pick, `_restoreQuality(list)` again (a saved 1440p60 or "highest" may only now be on offer).
- `quality` {name} (current rendition): index updated; with auto latency: `_playingQuality = name`; if NOT requested by the store within the last 5 s (`_qualityRequestWindow`) and it is a down-switch from the previous (`isQualityDownSwitch`) -> `_autoLatency.onQualityChanged()` + apply.
- `ad` {durationMs, startMs, endMs} (IVS `AdCue`): with auto latency `onAd(Duration(milliseconds: durationMs ?? 0))` + apply (never speed an ad up); a missing `durationMs` is 0 ms, clamped by the controller to the 60 s minimum hold.
- `latency` {ms, bufferMs, rate, lowLatency} once per second (nulls when the SDK cannot answer): ms < 0 or no frame yet -> ignored (IVS has no live position before the first frame; often 0) except: played and ms missing with auto latency -> `onMissingLatency()` + apply(nativeRate). Else `latencyMs = ms`, `latency = formatLatency(ms)`; if playing, add to `latencySampler` and bump `samplesVersion`. `judged = _isPlaying && !_recovering && !_jumping && status == playing`; `if (_liveEdge.onSample(ms, playing: judged)) _jumpToLive()`. With auto latency: `onSample(latencyMs, bufferMs, lowLatency, nativeRate, hold: _autoLatencyHold)`; if paused or covered mark disturbed; apply(nativeRate). If not playing stop here (paused: chat keeps the delay of the last moment played). Chat delay: `seconds = autoLatency ? chatDelaySecondsWithHysteresis(ms, last) : chatDelaySecondsFromLatency(ms)`; on change and with auto sync -> `syncedChatDelay = seconds`.
- `rebuffering`: `rebufferCount++`; with auto latency, a frame played and not recovering: `onStall()`, publish; if buffer is Ultra and not `_viewerStall` -> `_bufferFallback.onUltraRebuffer()`.
- `stats` (only while the panel is open): `_stats = IvsStats.fromEvent(e)` (never resurrect hidden stats).
- `error` {code, msg, errorType, source}: `kind = classifyIvsError(...)`; log `'IVS error: type code source -> kind'`; while recovering, non-fatal errors are the same incident -> ignored; `_lastErrorRefused = kind == refreshToken`; refused AND the token itself carried a refusal (`refusalFor(login) != null`) -> `_failExplained()` (a new token changes nothing); else `_applyRecovery(_recovery.onError(kind))`.

**Auto latency glue**
- `_autoLatencyHold`: paused or covered -> `paused`; recovering, jumping or status != playing -> `reconnecting`; not playing -> `buffering`; else null.
- `_viewerStall`: paused/covered, or disturbed within `_fallbackGrace = 10 s` (pause, resume, PiP, mini player, content gate, coming back to the app — stalls around those are not the network's and never count toward the Ultra fallback). `_markDisturbed()` sets `_disturbedAt = now`.
- `_holdAutoLatency(reason)`: mark disturbed; with auto latency `hold(reason)` + apply.
- `_applyAutoRate({nativeRate})`: `_sendWantedRate` unless disposed, then publish status (only on value change).
- `_sendWantedRate(nativeRate)` — only while PLAYING and a channel exists; `rate = controller.rate`:
  - rate > 1: skip if `_rateRefused` or already sent; reset resends; send; start the tick watchdog.
  - rate 1: `fast = nativeRate > 1.005`. If sent 1.0 and not fast -> (reset resends when a native rate is known) return. If sent is NaN (a failed 1.0x) and a non-fast native rate is reported -> sent = 1.0, return. If sent 1.0 or NaN (a resend): after `_quickResends = 2` resends, if still fast and not stopped -> `stop(stuckFast, rate: nativeRate)` and report to telemetry (`StateError('live rate stayed at $nativeRate after 1.0x')`, reason `'ivs auto latency rate'`) — one shared `_rateTroubleReported` flag covers this AND `_onRateFailed`: at most ONE rate-trouble record per stream across both kinds; then resend only every `_slowResend = 10 s`. Count the resend, record time, send 1.0.
- `_send` sets `_sentRate` and calls `setPlaybackRate {'rate'}`; `PlatformException`/`MissingPluginException` -> `_onRateFailed`: log, report (same shared once-per-stream flag); refused fast rate -> `_rateRefused = true` (never ask fast again this stream), sent back to 1.0 if it was that rate, `onRateRejected()`; refused 1.0 -> sent = NaN, `stop(notBackToNormal)`; publish (retried on the next tick, never looped). Native side clamps live rates to [1.0, 1.1] and replies `rate_rejected` on exception.
- `_watchTicks()`: one `Timer.periodic(autoLatencyWatchdog 1 s)`: `checkTicks()` -> apply; cancels itself when rate <= 1 or disposed.

**Recovery** (`_applyRecovery`):
- `IvsRetry(delay, freshToken)`: `_recovering = true`, status reconnecting, `retryAttempt = attemptsInWindow`; timer `delay` -> `_reload(epoch, freshToken)`.
- `IvsOffline`: recovering, `_confirmOffline(epoch)`.
- `IvsFallback`: recovering false; `_lastErrorRefused ? _failExplained() : _fail()`.
- `_reload(epoch, {freshToken})`: stale -> return; refused flag false; `getUsherUrl(forceFreshToken: freshToken)`; set url; `_loadSource`; recovering false. Errors: stale -> return; recovering false; `onError(retryable ? recoverable : offline)`.
- `_confirmOffline(epoch)`: first clears `_lastErrorRefused` (so an IvsOffline path never ends in `_failExplained`); then Helix `getStream`: 404 -> offline candidate; other ApiException or unexpected (reported) -> unknown. Stale -> return. Unknown -> recoverable retry. Offline but `_justWentLive` -> stay reconnecting and reload after 3 s outside the retry budget. Live after all -> store info, recoverable retry. Offline -> streamInfo null, status offline, paused; then `getChannel(userId)` for offline info (errors ignored).
- `_failExplained()`: cancel retry; `shownName` = stream userName / offline broadcasterName / login; notice from the token refusal (`StreamRefusalNotice(refusal, name)`); else, with a URL, `usherRefusalDetails(url).timeout(4 s)` (timeout logged, other errors reported 'usher reason'): a known usher error code -> refusal notice; else a free-text usher reason -> `StreamRefusalNotice(name, usherReason)` (logged in English). If still current -> `refusalNotice = notice` then `_fail()`.
- `_fail()`: cancel retry (a failed, maybe minimised player must not reload behind the user's back), bump epoch, recovering false, status failed, `onFatalError?.call()` (the channel screen falls back to the WebView).

**Content gate / focus**
- `_contentGate = Observable(ContentGate.clear)` (the auto-PiP autorun follows it). `setContentGate(gate)`: covering -> `_holdAutoLatency(paused)` and, only if PLAYING, held + `pause` (a stream still loading is held when it starts); clear and held -> mark disturbed, held false, `handlePausePlay()` if paused and not focus-suppressed. `_holdsPlayback = _focusSuppressed || covered`. `setFocusSuppressed(bool)` — a VOD opened on top keeps this stream silent (automatic playback after a load/reconnect is paused again).

- `prepareFloatingPlayer()` (before the floating window starts): disarm auto PiP, pause if playing. `floatingPlayerClosed({returned})`: re-apply auto PiP; if returned and paused -> play. `debugMarkAutoPipArmed()` (tests).
- `onPipEntered()` (@action): `isInPipMode = true`, disturbed. `onPipExited()`: false, disturbed, `_liveEdge.expedite()`. `setMinimized(bool)` (in-app mini player) -> disturbed. Auto latency keeps correcting in PiP and the mini player.

- `handleAppBackground()`: `trip = ++_backgroundTrip`; only if `_wantsHeadphonesForBackground` (not disposed, playing, not in PiP, auto PiP not armed, backgroundAudio && backgroundAudioHeadphonesOnly); waits `backgroundPauseDelay` 1 s; then if `headphonesConnected() == false` and still away -> pause. `handleAppResume()`: `_backgroundTrip++` (cancels a pending check), disturbed, `_liveEdge.expedite()`, chat-only -> force stream info update.

**Pause / live edge**
- `resumeAtLiveAfter = 10 s`: a paused live stream played again after >= 10 s resumes AT THE LIVE EDGE (fresh load) instead of where it stopped; shorter pauses resume in place (the speed-up absorbs a few seconds; a reload would cost a visible rebuffer).

- `_jumpToLive({resume})`: one at a time (`_jumping`); while recovering just `play` if resuming; fetch a URL (token reused while valid: one playlist request + short rebuffer); failure -> log type, `play` if resuming and current; if stale/recovering/a pause happened meanwhile (non-resume) -> abort; set url, `liveJumps++`; `_loadSource`; load failure -> recoverable recovery; finally `_jumping = false`.

### lib/screens/channel/stores/create_clip_store.dart

`CreateClipStore` (MobX) clips a live stream as the viewer: one Helix request, then a look every few seconds until the clip exists, at most a minute (Twitch's advice).
`enum CreateClipStatus { idle, needsLogin, checking (reading the login's scopes), missingScope (login from before clips: reconnecting grants clips:edit), creating, rendering (Twitch is making it; already editable on twitch.tv), ready, failed }`.
Constructor `({required TwitchApi twitchApi, required String broadcasterId, required bool Function() isLoggedIn, Future<ClipEligibilityRefusal?> Function()? checkEligibility, pollInterval = 5 s, renderTimeout = 60 s})`; `clipScope = 'clips:edit'`.
Observables (readonly): `status` (idle), `failure` (ClipCreateFailure?), `refusal` (ClipEligibilityRefusal?), `creation` (ClipCreation? {id, editUrl}), `thumbnailUrl`. `_busy` = checking/creating/rendering.
`start()` (@action; once at a time; ignored when disposed or busy): clears failure/refusal/creation/thumbnail; not logged in -> `needsLogin`, return. `checking`: `twitchApi.getTokenScopes()` (GET `https://id.twitch.tv/oauth2/validate`, quiet: no "session expired" prompt) — missing `clips:edit` -> `missingScope`; errors -> unknown, continue (`debugPrint('Clip scope check failed: <type>')`). Eligibility: `checkEligibility()` (GQL `CanCreateClipForChannelQuery`: `query CanCreateClipForChannelQuery($broadcasterId: ID!) { canCreateClip(broadcasterID: $broadcasterId) { isAllowed errorCode requiredFollowingLengthMinutes } }`, viewer's web token; errors -> null) — a refusal -> `failed` with `refusal`. `creating`: `twitchApi.createClip(broadcasterId)` = Helix `POST /clips?broadcaster_id=<id>` (quiet 401) -> `ClipCreation(id, editUrl only if https on twitch.tv or *.twitch.tv without userinfo)`; HTTP mapping 401 -> missingScope, 403 -> restricted, 404 -> offline, 400 -> notClippable, 429 -> rateLimited, other -> unknown; invalid id -> unknown. Success -> `rendering` + `_watch(id)`. `ClipCreateException(missingScope)` -> status `missingScope`; other reasons -> failed(reason); any other exception -> failed(unknown).
`_watch(id)`: `Timer.periodic(5 s)`; one look at a time (`looking` flag); `waited += 5000`; `twitchApi.getCreatedClip(id)` = Helix `GET /clips?id=<id>` (null while not yet made; thumbnail kept only if https); made -> cancel, thumbnail, `ready`; else when waited >= 60000 -> failed(unknown). Errors are logged (`Clip status check failed: <type>`) and retried next tick, but `waited` grows on every tick including failed ones, so a failed look at >= 60 s also ends in failed(unknown).
`dispose()`: disposed, cancel poll. All async steps check `_disposed` before writing.

PC: identical store (Rust), same endpoints.

- `viewerChanged()` (account known late on cold start, sign in/out): following = null, alsoLive = [], alsoLiveDone false, refresh (what was read belonged to the previous account).
- `refreshFollowing()`: needs a viewer id; `api.isFollowing(userId: viewer, broadcasterId: channelId)` (Helix `GET /channels/followed?user_id=&broadcaster_id=`); written only if not disposed and the viewer is unchanged (never one account's follows on another's screen); errors -> `debugPrint('Follow state unavailable: $error')` (the FULL error object, unlike most of this leaf). NOT gated by `_ready`: the screen's call after the viewer comes back from twitch.tv reads at once, even inside the 2 s head start; with no viewer it returns and leaves `following` as it was (possibly null).
- `wantAlsoLive()`: the tablet info area is on screen -> wanted; load if ready.
- `_loadAlsoLive()`: guards done/loading/disposed/no viewer; `api.getFollowedStreams(id: viewer, first: 7, quiet: true)` (Helix `GET /streams/followed`); account changed meanwhile -> ask again for the new one; else `alsoLive = unmodifiable(streams excluding this channel, take 6)`, done = true; errors logged (done stays false so the next refresh retries).
- `dispose()`: cancels the timer and disposes both notifiers.

### lib/screens/channel/stores/channel_events_store.dart

`ChannelEventsStore` (MobX) owns the real-time Hermes connection (`wss://hermes.twitch.tv/v1?clientId=<web client id>`, `HermesClient`) for the channel being watched, plus the GQL reads/writes of channel points, predictions, watch streak, drops, weekly rewards, hype chests, moments, resub sharing, community goals, leaderboards and unlockable emotes.
