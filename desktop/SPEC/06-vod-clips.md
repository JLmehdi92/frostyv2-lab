# 06 — VOD and clips (recorded video)

> Cross-spec decisions in [00-index.md](00-index.md) (section "Decisions") override any conflicting PC suggestion in this file.

Scope: `lib/screens/vod/**` and `lib/screens/clips/**` of ONSET (Flutter, Android-first), with their tests under `test/screens/vod/**` and `test/screens/clips/**`. ## Purpose

ONSET's mission is low-latency live, but every feature of the upstream client must survive, and recorded video is a large one. This area lets the viewer watch, inside the app and never in a browser:

1. **VODs** (past broadcasts `ARCHIVE`, highlights `HIGHLIGHT`, uploads `UPLOAD`) with a native player (Amazon IVS texture player on Android) and an automatic fallback to the Twitch web embed in a WebView (iOS, the "Twitch web" engine setting, or any native failure). Seek with a scrub bar that shows copyright-muted parts and chapters, ±10 s buttons and side double-taps, 0.5×–2× speeds, hold-for-2×, quality, volume, closed captions, screen lock, cast, picture-in-picture, fit/fill, rotation, go-to-time, copy link at the current time, share.
2. **Chat replay** synchronised with the video, rendered through the very same pipeline as live chat (badges, Twitch/7TV/BTTV/FFZ emotes, colours, mentions, blocked users and muted words) — read-only.
3. **Resume** where the viewer left (this device, the Twitch account, or where they left the live broadcast the VOD recorded), periodic saves, writes back to the Twitch account, progress bars and "Watched" chips in lists.
4. **Up Next** at the end of a VOD like the official app (next channel video or a similar one, 10 s countdown governed by the autoplay setting, Cancel, Play now, collection queues).
5. **Mature-content warnings** before a labelled VOD plays, and audience/paid-promotion pills.
6. **Video lists**: a channel's past broadcasts/highlights/uploads/clips screen, category video lists, with skeletons, empty/error states, pull to refresh, scroll-to-top.
7. **Clips**: the clip screen (media_kit/libmpv MP4 player, loop, quality with a 480p cap on metered networks, details, "Watch full video" at the clip's offset, chat replay of the source VOD in step, or the channel's other clips), the vertical **clips feed** (Home's Clips tab; phone pages / tablet stage + queue), a **story** player (portrait version of a clip, recaps), **My clips** (of my channel / made by me, custom date ranges, rename/delete), **comments** (read, post, like, eligibility), **download** (save/share the MP4), report.

Every Twitch call degrades gracefully: a failing native token/usher falls back to the web engine; a failing list shows a retryable error; chat replay failing becomes "unavailable" with retry; integrity refusals are final and said plainly.

## Behaviour and state

**Engine choice and fallback.** `resolvePlayerEngine(setting: playerEngine, isAndroid, forceWeb)`. IVS store: token preloaded while labels/history are fetched; `ready` rejects only when no playlist can be had; forbidden tokens (sub-only, geoblocked) become a `problem` state (no fallback, error view with Subscribe/Retry). `onFatalError`/`ready` failure → `_switchPlayer(forceWeb: true)` at `currentPositionForSave()`; `_forceWeb` sticks for the screen. Clips: ClipPlayerStore failure (no playback at all, or a second playback error after one fresh-token reopen) → WebVodStore on `clips.twitch.tv/embed` (once); the clips feed never falls back automatically (it says "can't play" and "Open clip" goes to the clip screen which does).

**Playback focus (one player at a time).** Each player screen closes the mini player, registers and claims `PlaybackFocus`; losing focus pauses (remembering "was playing or loading") and unfocuses the store (so an automatic start after a reload is paused again and auto-PiP is left to the top player); regaining focus (post-frame) resumes only if the mini player is not visible, reclaims PiP callbacks and re-syncs system bars. Pressing play while a channel plays in the mini player closes the mini player (VOD screen reaction). On dispose: system UI and orientation restored only if this screen was top; focus released last; wakelock disabled only if nobody else holds focus.

**Overlay visibility.** Visible at start; auto-hide after 5 s only while playing; tap on video toggles (hide only when playing); any seek or settings open shows it; paused/ended keep it visible; double-tap disambiguation never delays button taps (detector under the controls).

**Resume and progress.** `startPositionFor` (deep link > device −30 s > account as is > live −30 s, newer-by-2-min wins, near-end restart unless recording). `VodResumeController` saves every 20 s while playing, on pause/end, on app background, on close; not before playback actually started; writes the account position (`UpdateUserViewedVideo`, type VOD) when > 0. Repository: 300 entries, per-account keys, `changes` notifier feeds `VodWatchProgressMixin` lists. "Watched" rules for Up Next: <5 min → to the end, ≤50 min → 5 min, longer → 10 %.

**Up Next.** Not for recordings. On `ended`: look up once per screen (collection queue > channel videos > similar category videos), skip current/sub-only/watched; `autoplayAllowed` (always / Wi-Fi only (unknown network = wait) / never) chooses countdown vs waiting; countdown 10 s, held in PiP or while another player has focus (and restarted afterwards), restarted while covered; Cancel dismisses; replay/seek-back resets; Play now → `pushReplacement` (never stacks; only when this screen is on top). The next VOD goes through its own warning.

**Chat replay.** Position seconds drive it; seeks (>3 s forward, >1 s back) clear and refill with the 50 comments leading to the position (only from the covered stretch); prefetch 15 s × rate ahead; one request at a time, 300 ms gap after a busy page, 250 ms debounce while scrubbing; 1 s / 2 s backoff, 3 failures → unavailable (Try again), integrity → unavailable forever; 300 messages max (purge 60); eviction ±30 min / 20 000 comments; uploads have no chat (`notApplicable`, no network). The screen feeds `positionSeconds` and `playbackRate`; clips feed `vodOffsetSeconds + positionSeconds`.

**Content warning.** With `warnMatureContent`, the VOD's own labels (3 s timeout, fail-open) gate playback behind `ContentWarningCover`; accepting records `AcceptedContentWarnings` for the channel (never asked again for it, labels then shown as audience pills); the web engine auto-clicks Twitch's own interstitial only with that consent, otherwise our controls step aside (`webGateVisible`) so the viewer can tap it.

**Landscape chat.** Double-tap the middle third cycles side column → overlay hidden → overlay shown; divider drag sets `chatWidth` (no animation while dragging, 200 ms ease otherwise); tablet side chat uses `#0D0D0D` in dark.

**Clips feed.** Exactly two players alive (current focused/playing, next preloaded paused), the rest retired after the frame that stops drawing them; `loadMore` 3 clips before the end; 200 clips max; a stale kept list (left ≥10 min) restarts; `active=false` pauses and disables auto-PiP and wakelock; PiP toggles the shell via `onPipChanged`.

**Clip player.** Loop persisted (`clip_loop`); quality from `last_stream_quality` / highest-quality setting, capped at 480p on metered networks unless "default to highest"; one token refresh on error (errors in the next 2 s ignored; mpv bursts count once); seeks clamp to [0, duration]; quality change reopens at the same position, playing only if it was.

**Lists.** Channel videos: newest 100 per type, category: 100 most viewed, "Showing the N most recent/most viewed" footer when Twitch has more. Clips: top 100 per (period, sort) with chip cache, newest/oldest re-sorted locally, trending hidden with a custom range, "featured only" filter (channel lists, when known), reset chip when filtered, date range picker (2016…today, inclusive days).

## Settings

Read from `SettingsStore` (fields; defaults in parentheses). Persistence/UI of these settings belongs to the settings leaf; here is how this area uses them:

- `playerEngine` (`PlayerEngine.ivs`): IVS on Android, web otherwise; non-Android always web.
- `keepScreenAwake` (true): wakelock while a VOD/clip/feed holds focus (the story/recap screen always enables the wakelock).
- `autoPictureInPicture` (true): arms Android auto-PiP while playing and focused.
- `warnMatureContent` (true): content-warning gate for VODs and the web interstitial consent.
- `vodAutoplay` (`VodAutoplay.always`; `wifiOnly`, `never`): Up Next countdown vs waiting; legend text on tablets.
- `defaultToHighestQuality` (false): initial quality (IVS restore, clip pick) and disables the clips' metered 480p cap.
- `captionSize` (`CaptionSize.medium`), `captionBackground` (`CaptionBackground.black`): caption rendering in `VodVideo`; caption on/off and track live in `CaptionStore`.
- Landscape chat: `fullScreen` (false), `fullScreenChatOverlay` (false), `fullScreenChatOverlayOpacity` (0.5), `chatWidth` (0.2, divider max 0.6), `landscapeChatLeftSide` (false), `landscapeCutout` (`none`; `left`/`right`/`both` drop the SafeArea on that side), `landscapeForceVerticalChat` (false; VODs only — clips ignore it).
- `accentColor`: seed of the dark overlay theme (landscape chat overlay, clips feed).
- `streamLanguage` ('' = all): clips feed language filter.
- `clipDiscovery` (via `featuredClipsOnly(context)` = `== ClipDiscovery.featured`): channel clip lists keep featured clips only.
- Chat replay rendering (shared with live chat): `showTwitchEmotes`, `showTwitchBadges`, `show7TVEmotes`, `showBTTVEmotes`, `showBTTVBadges`, `showFFZEmotes`, `showFFZBadges`, `mutedWords` ([]), `matchWholeWord`, `timestampType` (`disabled`; any other value shows the VIDEO time `M:SS`/`H:MM:SS`), `emoteScale`, `badgeScale`, `animateEmotes` (true), `showGifs` (true), `animateGifs` (true), `nameColorContrast` (computed: `increaseChatContrast` (default false) ? `highNameColorContrast` = 7.0 : `readableNameColors` (default true) ? `readableNameColorContrast` = 4.5 : null), `launchUrlExternal`, `messageSpacing` (8.0), `showChatMessageDividers` (false), `messageScale` (text scaler), `fontSize` (12.0).

SharedPreferences keys owned or written here:
- `vod_resume_v1` — JSON map of resume entries (see vod_resume.dart; 300 max, per-account keys `viewer|vod`).
- `clip_loop` — bool, clip loop toggle (default false), shared by native and web clip players.
- `last_stream_quality` — string, written when a quality is picked in the VOD/clip settings sheet (shared with live), read by IVS VOD restore and clip pick.

## Network and data

All GraphQL goes to `https://gql.twitch.tv/gql` with `Client-ID: kimne78kx3ncx6brgo4mv6wki5h1ko` (Twitch web client id), POST body `{operationName, query, variables}` (full query documents, no persisted hashes). A response with `errors` throws (`VodApiException`/`ClipApiException`, `isIntegrity` when a message contains "integrity"); no `data` throws. Viewer-authenticated calls add `Authorization: OAuth <twitch web token>` (only token, history and comment/account mutations).

VOD (`TwitchVodService`, used here):
- `getVideo(id)` — `query Video($id: ID!) { video(id: $id) { <videoFields> } }`; null video → not found. `videoFields` = `id title lengthSeconds createdAt viewCount status broadcastType previewThumbnailURL(width: 640, height: 360) game { id name } owner { id login displayName } broadcast { id } self { isRestricted }`.
- `getChannelVideos(login, type)` — `ChannelVideos($login, $type: BroadcastType, $first: 100)`: `user(login) { id login displayName videos(first, type, sort: TIME) { edges { node {…} } pageInfo { hasNextPage } } }`; no user → NotFound. Types `ARCHIVE`/`HIGHLIGHT`/`UPLOAD` (null = all, used by Up Next).
- `getCategoryVideos(gameId)` — `CategoryVideos($id, $first: 100, $sort: VIEWS)`: `game(id) { id videos(first, sort) {…} }`; numeric id only.
- `getCollectionVideos(id)` — `CollectionVideos($id)`: `collection(id) { items(first: 100) { edges { node { ... on Video {…} } } } }`; id `^[A-Za-z0-9_-]{1,64}$`; errors → [].
- `getComments(vodId, offsetSeconds)` — `VideoComments($id: ID!, $offset: Int) { video(id) { comments(contentOffsetSeconds: $offset) { edges { node { id contentOffsetSeconds commenter { id login displayName } message { fragments { text emote { emoteID } } userBadges { setID version } userColor } } } pageInfo { hasNextPage } } } }`. Pagination model: by OFFSET only — each request returns ~50 comments starting at/just before the offset; `hasNextPage` used only to decide coverage; cursors are integrity-gated for anonymous clients so are never used; the client advances by asking the first uncovered second.
- `getContentLabels(id)` — `VideoContentLabels`: `video { contentClassificationLabels { id } }` (video's own labels).
- `vodHasPaidPromotion(id)` — `BrandedContentVodMetadataQuery($vodId)`: `video { contentPolicyProperties { hasBrandedContent } }`; errors → false.
- `getMutedSegments(id)` — `VideoMutedSegments`: `video { muteInfo { mutedSegmentConnection { nodes { offset duration } } } }`; errors → [].
- `getChapters(id)` — `VideoChapters`: `video { moments(momentRequestType: VIDEO_CHAPTER_MARKERS, types: [GAME_CHANGE]) { edges { node { positionMilliseconds durationMilliseconds description details { ... on GameChangeMomentDetails { game { id displayName boxArtURL(width: 40, height: 53) } } } } } } }`; sorted; errors → [].
- `getViewingHistory(id)` (needs web token) — `VideoViewingHistory`: `video { self { viewingHistory { position updatedAt } } }`; 3 refusals per token stop asking; 401 marks the token refused and triggers renewal.
- `updateViewedVideo(videoId, type: VOD, positionSec)` (needs web token + numeric viewer id, position > 0) — `mutation UpdateUserViewedVideo($input: UpdateUserViewedVideoInput!) { updateUserViewedVideo(input: $input) { video { id } } }` with `input {userID, videoID, videoType: "VOD", position}`.
- Playback token — `query VodToken($id: ID!) { videoPlaybackAccessToken(id: $id, params: {platform: "web", playerBackend: "mediaplayer", playerType: "site"}) { value signature } }`, sent with the viewer's web token when available (anonymous fallback; after 2 viewer-token refusals the store asks anonymously). Cached per `(vod, sha256(webToken)[0..16])`; forbidden tokens are not cached; `preloadToken`, `invalidateToken`.
- Usher URL — `https://usher.ttvnw.net/vod/<id>.m3u8?allow_source=true&allow_audio_only=true&supported_codecs=av1,h265,h264&playlist_include_framerate=true&p=<µs mod 10 000 000>&sig=<signature>&token=<url-encoded token>`. The token's `chansub`/geoblock restrictions are decoded first (`VodAccessInfo`) → `VodForbiddenException(reason)` (`'geoblocked'` → geoBlocked else subscriberOnly).
- `diagnoseVodPlaylist(url)` (usher host only): GET; 2xx → null (fine); 404 → notFound; JSON `error_code`: `vod_manifest_restricted` → subscriberOnly, `content_geoblocked` → geoBlocked, `anonymizer_blocked` → proxyDetected, `cellular_geoblocked` → wifiRequired, `no_cdm_specified` → unsupportedDevice, `nauth*` → tokenRejected, else unknown.
- Web engine: `https://player.twitch.tv/?video=v<id>&autoplay=true&muted=false&parent=onset[&time=1h2m3s]`.

Clips (`TwitchClipService`): `clipFields` = `id slug title viewCount createdAt durationSeconds thumbnailURL videoOffsetSeconds curator { id login displayName } broadcaster { id login displayName } game { id name } video { id }`.
- `getTopClips(cursor?, language?)` — `TopClipsFeed($first: 20, $after: Cursor, $languages: [String!])`: `topClips(first, after, languages) { edges { node {…} } pageInfo { hasNextPage endCursor } }`; cursor paging (works anonymously); `languages: [streamLanguage]` when set.
- `getChannelClips(login, period, sort)` — `ChannelClips($login, $first: 100, $period: ClipsPeriod, $sort: ClipsSort)`: `user(login) { clips(first, criteria: {period, sort}) {…} }` (a variant also asks `isFeatured`; `featuredKnown` false when it could not). ## Wide and tablet layout

"Tablet" = `context.isLargeScreen`: a `LargeScreenScope` override if one is above, else the window's shortest side ≥ `kLargeScreenShortestSide` = 600 dp (re-evaluated on every resize/rotation, so a running screen can switch layouts live). Differences:
- VodScreen portrait: details panel (with channel card and action pills, no legend) between the video and the chat (Flexible, scrolls), chat header 56 dp with hairline and `large` replay text. Landscape side-by-side: the video column is the tablet video column (16:9 video at the top, height `min(width·9/16, height − 120)`, details panel with legend below), chat column with a hairline on the video side and `#0D0D0D` in dark theme; full-screen modes (overlay/hidden) still available. Upload without chat: tablet keeps the video column with details unless full screen.
- VodOverlay: tablet metrics when large screen and the player is ≥600 wide and ≥300 tall (disc 68, skip 52, edges 10, ringed avatar 14, title 15/700, kind pill "Past broadcast", 13 time); compact metrics below 190 tall regardless of device.
- Lists: `AdaptiveSliverList.videos(tiles: true)` grid of tiles (`VideoCardLayout.tile`: picture on top radius 14, avatar 16 beside the title when the channel is named, 10 dp padding), tile skeletons; row thumbnails widen up to 240 dp (36 % of the row) on wider rows.
- ClipScreen: tablet behaves like a phone (portrait: player + details/chat; landscape: video alone).
- Clips feed: stage + queue layout (side by side at ≥1100 dp content width, else stacked), stage width `max(240, min(byHeight, side ? min(800, width − 3·gutter − 340) : width − 2·gutter))`, swipe on the stage (|v| ≥ 300) changes clip, tap in the queue; skeleton box up to 800 wide with radius 20. Switching layouts keeps the current clip.
- Phone held sideways: feed shows full-bleed video with bottom details; VodScreen goes landscape immersive.
- All horizontal paddings add the system insets (notches) on rows, cards, headers and the filter bar.

## PC equivalent

Target: Rust + Slint UI + libmpv, Windows first (macOS/Linux later). Nothing is dropped; mobile-only affordances get desktop counterparts.

- **Two engines, as on Android (decision D1 in 00-index.md): libmpv is the native engine** for VODs AND clips (it replaces the IVS texture player), **and the Twitch web embed in WebView2 (wry) is kept** as the automatic fallback and the "Twitch web" engine setting — `WebVodStore` and its test are ported, same triggers as Android. VOD: mpv opens the usher URL (`…/vod/<id>.m3u8?allow_source=true&allow_audio_only=true&supported_codecs=av1,h265,h264&playlist_include_framerate=true&p=…&sig=…&token=…`) with `start=<seconds>`; HLS variants from the master playlist become the quality list (`Auto` = mpv `hls-bitrate=max`/adaptive, a named rendition = select that variant by `NAME`/resolution, including "audio only"); rate via `speed` (only the 7 offered speeds), seek via `seek <abs> absolute+exact` with the same optimistic-position/1.5 s tolerance/3 s timeout logic, observed properties `time-pos`, `duration` (ignore ≤0/huge while recording), `demuxer-cache-time` (buffered), `paused-for-cache` (buffering), `eof-reached` (ended), `pause`. Keep the start-position guard (`awaitingStart`): ignore positions until the first `playback-restart` after `loadfile`, so a resume point is never overwritten by 0. Errors: `end-file` with `reason=error` → classify like `classifyIvsError` (HTTP 403 → refresh token, 404/410 → diagnose usher, network → retry with `IvsRecoveryPolicy` delays) → diagnose → problem state or retry; the automatic fallback to the WebView2 engine follows the Android rules exactly (`WebVodStore`), and the problem/error view stays reachable. Captions: mpv `sid` tracks (CEA-608/WebVTT in the HLS) drive `CaptionStore`; render with our own caption box (size/background settings) rather than mpv's OSD.
- **Clips**: mpv with the signed MP4 URL (never logged), `loop-file=inf|no` for the loop toggle (persisted `clip_loop`), volume 0–100, the same one-fresh-token reopen at the current position with 2 s error grace, quality list from renditions with the 480p cap when Windows reports a metered connection (`NetworkCostType` via WinRT `NetworkInformation`; unknown = unmetered for clips, possibly-mobile for VOD autoplay "Wi-Fi only").
- **Rendering**: mpv render API (OpenGL/D3D11 via `mpv_render_context`) into a Slint texture/underlay; fit = letterbox 16:9, fill = crop (`panscan=1.0` or custom crop), never distort. Several mpv instances allowed simultaneously only for the clips feed (current + preloaded next, the next created with `pause=yes`); all others disposed after the frame that stops drawing them.

### Known Android defects

Real bugs in the Android code; the PC port fixes them unless noted (behaviour otherwise identical):

1. **Go to time with an unknown length** (`go_to_time_dialog.dart`): `_target` refuses `parsed > length`; when the player has not reported a duration and the VOD metadata length is 0, `length` is `Duration.zero`, so only `0`/`0:00` is accepted, Go stays disabled and the error says "between 0:00 and 0:00". PC: when length ≤ 0, accept any parsed time (seek clamps it) and omit the upper bound from the error.
2. **Go to time cannot be submitted with Enter**: the field has no `onSubmitted`. PC: Enter submits when valid (Esc cancels).
3. **Phantom "Open channel" button in the overlay** (`vod_overlay.dart` top row): the Semantics button labelled `clipOpenChannel(channel: ownerDisplayName)` is built even with an empty owner, so screen readers announce "Open " and activation does nothing. PC: only expose the channel button (and its accessible name) when ownerId and ownerLogin are known; otherwise the title is plain text.
4. **Story screen never auto-advances with loop on** (`clip_story_screen.dart` + `clip_player_store.dart`): the persisted `clip_loop` makes the backend loop, `completed` never fires, so stories/recaps stall on the first clip. PC: story players ignore the global loop (`loop-file=no`) without changing the saved preference.
5. **Picked date range shown one day too long** (`clip_filter_bar.dart`): `pickClipRange` stores `end = picked.end + 1 day` (exclusive bound) and both `clipRangeLabel` and `clipsEmptyText` format that `end`: Jan 1–Jan 5 reads "Jan 1 – Jan 6" / "No clips between Jan 1 and Jan 6". PC: display `end − 1 day` in both strings; keep the exclusive bound for the Helix request.
6. **Short videos list cannot be refreshed** (`channel_videos_tab.dart`): the list state's CustomScrollView has default physics, so on ChannelVideosScreen a channel with few videos never triggers pull-to-refresh. PC: the list is always refreshable (F5, refresh button, or pull with AlwaysScrollable-equivalent behaviour).
7. **Clip channel row that does nothing** (`clip_info_panel.dart`): the row/Semantics button appear when broadcasterLogin is non-empty but `_openChannel` also needs broadcasterId, so with an empty id it is announced "Open <channel>" and a tap is a no-op. PC: show the row as a button only when id and login are both known (plain text otherwise), and pass the login as the name when the display name is empty.
8. **"Yesterday" is 24–48 h ago, not the previous calendar day** (`formatTimeAgo`): a video from 23:00 yesterday seen at 08:00 reads "9 hours ago" and one from 47 h ago reads "Yesterday". Kept as is for parity unless the owner decides otherwise (listed so it is a deliberate choice, not an accident).

`_loadMetadata()` algorithm: (1) if `widget.vod` is null, `service.getVideo(vodId)`; exception → `_loadProblem = unknown`; null → `_loadProblem = notFound`; return. (2) If no test factory and the resolved engine is IVS, `service.preloadToken(vod.id)` fire-and-forget (token cached for the player while labels are awaited). (3) Starts `_contentWarningLabels(vod)` concurrently. (4) `resume = widget.startAt == null`; when resuming: `server = _accountPosition(vod)` (Twitch viewing history, 3 s timeout → null, errors → null), `live = LiveProgressRepository.shared.forVod(vod, viewer)` (position the viewer reached while watching the live broadcast this VOD recorded; errors → null), `saved = await _resumeRepository.entry(vod.id, viewer)`. (5) `startAt = startPositionFor(saved, deepLink: widget.startAt, server, live, lengthSec: vod.lengthSeconds, nearEndRestarts: !vod.isRecording)` (see vod_resume). `_contentWarningLabels`: returns `[]` when `warnMatureContent` is off or the owner's warning was already accepted (`AcceptedContentWarnings.has(ownerId)`); else `service.getContentLabels(vod.id).timeout(3 s)` passed through `contentLabelIds` (ids, named in the current language later); any failure → `[]` (fail open). ### lib/screens/vod/vod_resume.dart

`VodResumeEntry` (immutable): `positionSec`, `lengthSec`, `updatedAt` (DateTime?, null when unknown), `finished` (default false; watched to nearly the end → next play starts over). `progress`: 1 when finished; 0 when `lengthSec <= 0`; else `(positionSec/lengthSec).clamp(0, 1)` (list progress bars).

`VodResumeRepository` — singleton `shared` (private constructor); static `changes = ValueNotifier<int>(0)` bumped after each successful save so lists refresh progress bars. Storage: SharedPreferences key `'vod_resume_v1'`, one JSON object mapping entry key → `{"p": positionSec, "l": lengthSec, "u": updatedAt microseconds since epoch UTC, "f": 1 when finished}`. Entry key `'<viewer or empty>|<vodId>'` (`'|123'` logged out); legacy entries keyed by the bare VOD id (no `|`) stay readable by every account. Limits: `_maxEntries = 300` (most recently updated kept), `_minPosition = 30` (saves before 30 s are ignored), `_endMargin = 30` (within 30 s of the end → finished). All saves are serialized on `_queue` (read-modify-write chain; `resetQueue()` for tests). `_read()`: missing → `{}`; JSON not a Map → `{}`; entries kept only when key is String and value is a Map with int `p`, `l`, `u` (`f` kept only when == 1); decode error → `{}`. `_toEntry`: `updatedAt = DateTime.fromMicrosecondsSinceEpoch(u, isUtc: true)`.

`entry(vodId, {viewer})`: awaits the queue, reads, returns `all['viewer|vodId'] ?? all[vodId]`. `snapshot({viewer})`: awaits queue; map of all legacy (no `|`) entries plus all entries whose key starts with `'<viewer>|'`, keyed by vod id (account entries override legacy ones). `save(vodId, {positionSec, lengthSec, viewer, recording=false})` chained on the queue: position < 30 → return; `finished = !recording && lengthSec > 0 && positionSec >= lengthSec - 30`; stores `p = finished ? 0 : positionSec`, `l`, `u = clock.now()` µs, `f:1` if finished; if > 300 entries, sorts keys by `u` ascending and removes the oldest surplus; writes JSON; `changes.value++`. Errors are caught and logged ("VOD resume save failed"); never thrown.

`_sameSaveSlack = 2 minutes`. `startPositionFor({saved, deepLink, server (ViewingHistory), live (LiveProgressEntry), lengthSec = 0, nearEndRestarts = true})`: deepLink non-null → it wins verbatim. Candidates in priority order: saved (skipped when finished and `!nearEndRestarts`) as `(finished ? 0 : positionSec − 30, updatedAt)`; server as `(positionSec, updatedAt)` (as is, no rewind); live as `(offsetSec − 30, updatedAt)`. None → zero. Walk: start with the first; a later candidate replaces the current best only when both timestamps are known and the candidate's is more than 2 minutes after the best's (watched elsewhere since, or Twitch missed the live position); otherwise the order stands. Negative start → 0. length = `lengthSec > 0 ? lengthSec : saved?.lengthSec ?? 0`; if `nearEndRestarts && length > 0 && start >= length − 30` → zero (restart). A recording VOD passes `nearEndRestarts = false`.

`VodResumeController({vodId, player (closure returning current controller, which changes on fallback), repository, viewer?, report? (Future<bool> Function(int) — writes to the Twitch account), recording=false, fallbackLengthSec=0, interval=20 s})`. `_started` guard: nothing is saved until the status was `playing` at least once (before that the position is the rewound start, and saving would drift the resume point back on every quick open/close). `start()`: periodic timer each 20 s saving when the player exists and is not paused; MobX reaction on `player()?.status`: `playing` → `_started = true`; `paused` or `ended` → save. `save()`: no player or not started → return; `at = await currentPositionForSave()`; null → return; length = `p.duration.inSeconds` or fallback; in parallel: `repository.save(vodId, positionSec, lengthSec, viewer, recording)` and, when report exists and `at.inSeconds > 0`, `report(at)` with errors swallowed/logged ("VOD position report failed"). `cancel()` stops timer and reaction without saving. `close()` = cancel + one last save.

### lib/screens/vod/vod_comment_cache.dart

Replayed chat cache indexed by second, with ranges of seconds known complete. Twitch answers an offset with a page of ~50 comments starting at (or just before) that offset; there is no anonymous way to page within one second (cursors are integrity-gated), so a second with more comments than a page loses the remainder.

Internal: `_bySecond: Map<int, List<VodComment>>`, `_seenKeys: Set<String>` (comment `key`), `_covered: List<(int,int)>` sorted merged inclusive ranges. `length` = number of seen keys.

`addPage({requestedSecond, page (VodCommentPage: comments, hasNextPage), videoLengthSeconds})`: groups comments by `offsetSeconds`, tracks min/max offset (initialised to requestedSecond), merges each second; coverage end = `hasNextPage ? maxOffset − 1 : videoLengthSeconds` (the page may stop mid-second; the last page covers to the end), clamped to ≤ length and ≥ requestedSecond (a page stuck in one busy second still covers that second so replay moves on); start = `minOffset < requestedSecond ? minOffset + 1 : requestedSecond` (an earlier first second may be cut); `_cover(start, end)`.

`_merge(second, fromPage)`: no cached list → keep only unseen comments (if any) in page order. Cached exists and no key overlaps → append unseen page comments after the cached ones. Overlap → the page's order is authoritative (it saw the second from earlier): result = page comments (dedup, all marked seen) followed by cached-only comments.

`isCovered(s)`; `firstUncoveredFrom(s)` = s if uncovered else range end + 1. `commentsIn(fromExclusive, toInclusive)` → comments of seconds in (from, to], seconds ascending, per-second order kept. `lastN(second, n)`: from = start of the covered range containing second (or second itself if uncovered) — an earlier part of the video must not pass for what led up to it; walks seconds descending from `second` to `from`, collecting reversed lists until ≥ n, returns the newest n in oldest-first order.

`evictFarFrom(second, {keepSeconds = 1800, maxComments = 20000})`: keeps a window [second−keep, second+keep], removing other seconds (and their keys) and trimming/removing covered ranges to the window; if still more than maxComments and keep > 0, halves keep and repeats (integer division until 0). Forgotten seconds are no longer covered so they are refetched when needed. `_rangeOf` linear search over sorted ranges. `_cover` inserts, sorts by start and merges ranges that overlap or are adjacent (`start <= last.end + 1`).

ClipsFeedStore(clipService, language: settings.streamLanguage)`; `_pageController` (phone only; created at the shown clip where the phone layout is built, disposed post-frame when switching to the tablet layout); `_pipKey`; `_players: Map<int, VodPlayerController>` (current and next by index); `_playersVersion = ValueNotifier(0)` (bumped whenever `_players` changes so every video slot — including outgoing pages and stages — redraws); `_retiring` set (players let go, disposed after the frame that stops drawing them); `_index = min(store.position, clips.length)` (the end page is one past the last clip); `_started` (the clip last started as current — a later sync when more clips arrive must not restart it after the viewer paused it); `_inPip`; `_playingBeforeFocusLoss`; `_stageDirection = 1` (tablet stage slide direction; 1 = to a later clip); reaction disposers.

`_disableAutoPip()`: the injected callback, else on Android `SimplePip().setAutoPipMode(autoEnter: false)` (a player only turns auto-PiP off when disposed with focus; with no clip playing — end page, feed closing — the feed does it).

initState: if `store.stale` → `store.clear()` (a kept list left > 10 min ago starts over from the first clip); close the mini player; PlaybackFocus register(onLost: `_suspend`, onRegained) + claim; wakelock if keepScreenAwake; reaction on `store.clips.length` → `_syncPlayers()`; reaction on `miniPlayer.visible`: when it becomes hidden, post-frame (with `ensureVisualUpdate`) reclaim PiP callbacks if still mounted, mini still hidden and the feed holds focus (the mini window's PipWidget had taken the PiP events; but if the mini window hid because it was restored into its channel or a VOD/clip opened, the events belong to that screen); if the list is empty `store.load()`, else `_syncPlayers(rebuild: false)` (a kept list opens where it was, no reload).

didUpdateWidget: on `active` change: true → `_onFocusRegained()`; false → `_suspend()` and, if holding focus, disable auto-PiP (Android must not shrink a page over the feed into PiP) and disable the wakelock if keepScreenAwake.

`_createPlayer(clip, focused)`: factory (widget's or debug) else `ClipPlayerStore(slug, clipService, settings, knownLength: clip.duration, focused)`; `ready.catchError` swallowed (a failed player shows "can't play"; "Open clip" offers the web fallback — no automatic web fallback in the feed).

`_syncPlayers({rebuild = true})`: wanted = {index if < length, index+1 if < length}. Every other player: removed, unfocused, paused, added to `_retiring`, disposed in a post-frame callback (with `ensureVisualUpdate`) — a page/stage still sliding out keeps drawing its picture, never a disposed player. Bump `_playersVersion`. For each wanted index: `start = current && hasFocus && active && _started != index`; missing → create with `focused: start` (the next clip is created unfocused: preloaded paused); existing and start → `setFocused(true)` + `play()`; if start `_started = index`. If the current index is not wanted (end page) and the feed holds focus → disable auto-PiP. `setState` when rebuild.

`_onPageChanged(index)`: same index → ignore (phone pages recreated at the stage's clip); previous player unfocused + paused; `_stageDirection = sign(index − old)`; `_index = store.position = index`; `_syncPlayers()`; if `index >= clips.length − 3` → `store.loadMore()` (prefetch three clips before the end).

`_goTo(index)`: clamp to [0, length]; same → ignore; phone with clients → `animateToPage(target, duration motionDuration(360 ms), curve OnsetMotion.standard)`; else (tablet) `Haptics.selection()` + `_onPageChanged(target)` (instant).

`_suspend()`: current player unfocused; mark `_playingBeforeFocusLoss = true` if playing or loading (a second reason to pause keeps the flag); pause if playing. `_onFocusRegained()`: post-frame: stop if unmounted, not active, or not holding focus (still covered); if the mini player is hidden and a current player exists: if it was never started (`_started != _index`, made while not in front) → `_syncPlayers()`, else focus and play if it was playing; reclaim PiP callbacks when the mini is hidden and focus held (the screen above had its own PipWidget); clear the flag; wakelock if keepScreenAwake. Then `ensureVisualUpdate()` so the callback runs even with nothing to redraw.

`_video(index)`: `ValueListenableBuilder(_playersVersion)`: a `VideoSurfacePlayer` → `buildVideo(fill: false)`; no player but a thumbnail → `OnsetCachedNetworkImage(contain, error → empty)`; else empty expanded box.

`_videoBox(index, large)`: Stack(expand): black; the thumbnail (contain) under a player that is not a VideoSurfacePlayer (the picture until the player draws); Semantics(container, label `l.clipsPlayerSemantics`) opaque GestureDetector(onDoubleTap: player.togglePlay) with RepaintBoundary `_video(index)`; Observer when a player exists: loading/buffering → centred `CircularProgressIndicator(color: accent)`; failed/problem → centred padded (16) `l.clipsCannotPlay` (14 w600 textPrimaryDark, centred); paused and not ended → IgnorePointer centred `_PausedMark(size: large ? 76 : 64)`; else nothing; duration pill top-right (right/top 14 large, 8 phone) IgnorePointer `OverlayPill(height 26/22, radius 7/6, padding 9/7, colour 0x99000000)` with `formatVodTime(clip.duration)` (13/11.5 w600 tabular).

`_progress(index, height, track)`: RepaintBoundary SizedBox(height) track colour; Observer: played = position/duration (0 when duration ≤ 0) clamped; `FractionallySizedBox(widthFactor: played)` accent. Phone: height 2 track `0x1FFFFFFF`; tablet stage: height 3 track `0x24FFFFFF`.

`_openClip(clip)` → `ClipScreen.open(context, slug, clip)` (the full clip screen with details, chat replay, web fallback).

Portrait: `videoTop = padding.top + 24`, `videoHeight = width × 9/16`, `room = maxHeight − padding.bottom`, `showNext = next != null && room − (videoTop + videoHeight + 210) >= 110`.
