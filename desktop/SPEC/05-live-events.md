# 05 — Live events: community highlights, hype train, celebrations

> Cross-spec decisions in [00-index.md](00-index.md) (section "Decisions") override any conflicting PC suggestion in this file.

Scope: everything under `lib/screens/channel/community_highlights/**`, `lib/screens/channel/hype_train/**` and `lib/screens/channel/celebrations/**` of the ONSET Flutter app (repo `frostyv2-lab`), plus their tests under `test/screens/channel/{community_highlights,hype_train,celebrations}/**`. Shared models (`lib/models/community_highlights.dart`, `community_highlight_event.dart`, `hype_train.dart`, `hype_train_event.dart`, `celebration.dart`, `pinata.dart`, `raid_arrival.dart`), services (`TwitchCommunityHighlightsService`, `TwitchHypeTrainService`, `TwitchRealtimeClient`) and the chat-side gift-bomb tracker / incoming-raid parser belong to other leaves; they are summarized here only as far as this area consumes them. Generated MobX files (`*.g.dart`) are not ported literally: their observable semantics are described in each store section.

## Purpose

This area renders the "live events" Twitch layers over a channel, all of them independent of the video engine (native HLS or WebView fallback) and of the chat renderer:

1. **Community highlights** — the card stack above chat that Twitch web shows: a **poll** (with in-app voting when signed in, else "Vote on Twitch"), a **prediction** (outcomes, shares, return ratios, top predictor; predicting opens twitch.tv), a **pinned chat message** (rendered exactly like chat; moderators can unpin; any viewer can hide it permanently), an **outgoing raid** (this channel raids another: countdown ring, dotted track crossed by ONSET's rocket, "Go now" / "Stay here", automatic go-along when the raid leaves, "Heading to" full-video overlay), a **gift bomb banner** (≥5 gifted subs: gift box art in a filling ring, burst on the last gift, draining bar) and an **incoming raid pin** (another channel raids this one, 6.5 s). Twitch's recency stack rules decide which card shows; paging "2 / 3" and a "New …" pill handle several at once. In landscape with hidden chat, a small pill over the video stands in, and a bottom sheet shows the details.
2. **Hype train** — a faithful re-creation of Twitch's hype train banner (all variants: regular, golden Kappa, treasure, community, mythic, shared/multi-channel, all-time-high record approaching/reached, piñata details) with Twitch's official step timings: approaching banner with check circles and a time-window bar, level/percent/countdown banner whose progress fill is the background, scripted announcement sequences (marquees with emotes, "Level n complete!", instructions, large contributions, coin unlock, rocket launch, mythic roulette and reveal, trophy burst, merge auras, rainbow sweeps, speedlines), a details sheet, a collapsible pill, a landscape video pill, and a 20 s end strip. ONSET recolours Twitch's purple to its own green ramp and gives regular/shared trains per-level colours (green → teal → mint gradient → lime → orange → flowing lime/orange) with text that turns colour exactly at the fill edge.
3. **Celebrations** — Twitch's `alert_sets_celebration` events (fireworks, rain, float, flamethrower, extinction event; area chat/video/everywhere; five intensities) drawn with ONSET's own deterministic particle engine and spark art (never Twitch's Lottie), non-interactive, fading in and out, ending when hidden or backgrounded; or, in "Subtle" mode, turned into one chat line. Also ONSET's rocket SVG (used by raids) and the gift box/spark PNG art (used by the gift bomb pin).

Everything here is anonymous/read-only except three viewer actions (vote in a poll, leave/re-join a raid, unpin as a moderator), and every failure degrades to "nothing shown" — never an error over the stream. The PC port must keep every one of these behaviours, timings and visuals.

## Screens and layouts

All of these live on the **channel screen** (outside this area: `lib/screens/channel/channel.dart`, `channel_top_overlays.dart`), which owns one `CommunityHighlightsStore` and one `HypeTrainStore` per channel and places the widgets:

- **Above chat (portrait, chat-only, vertical landscape)**: `HypeTrainSlot` (full banner) stacked with `HighlightSlot`. On a large screen the banner is at most 560 dp wide and the full banner needs a chat ≥480 dp wide (constants in channel_top_overlays.dart).
- **Landscape side/overlay chat**: `HypeTrainSlot(compact: true)` (one-line banner, short sequences, details open as a sheet via `onOpenDetails`) and `HighlightSlot(compact: true)` (cards stay collapsed; a tap opens `showHighlightDetails`). Compact also applies under a running hype train on a phone and while typing.
- **Landscape full screen with hidden chat**: `HypeTrainVideoPill` and `HighlightVideoPill` over the video; taps open details sheets.
- **Landscape keyboard on a phone**: hype train placement `none` (hidden).
- **Over the video**: `RaidOverlayLayer` (never in the PiP window) and `CelebrationOverlay` (aligned to the chat or video region by the celebration's area).

Community highlight card anatomy (`HighlightCardShell`): 16 dp radius card in the sheet colour with a 0.5 dp ring and a soft shadow (black 35%/8%, y+10, blur 24); header row = 26 dp rounded tile with the kind glyph, title (13 w700), live-region status (12.5, tabular, ≤2 lines), 40×44 rotating chevron, 40×44 close; headline (15 w600, 1.4); animated body; optional footer. Bars are 36 dp tall with 10 dp radius. Raid card: lime-ringed 16 dp card with gradient, RAID eyebrow, 44 dp countdown ring, 32 dp track, 44 dp buttons. Gift bomb pin: 56 dp (wide) or 44 dp (one line), 12 dp radius, 40/28 dp ring slot, 2 dp drain bar. Incoming raid pin: 54 dp, 14 dp radius, 34 dp avatar with rocket.

Hype train anatomy: banner 74 dp at text scale 1 (`htBannerHeight` = 74 × clamp(textScale, 1, 2)), radius 12 (level colours) or 6, progress fill as background, level pill (black 80–86%, radius 20, "LVL n" 14 w600), title 18 w700, subtitle 14 w400 (2–3 lines then shrink to 80%), countdown 14 over percentage 24 w700 (≤40% of width, scaled down), 24 dp chevron; announcement layer covering the whole banner; optional 0.5 dp hairline. Approaching banner: float card, title/subtitle, green events pill (train icon + 2 or 3 check circles), 3 dp bar, 4 dp side mark while expanded. Details sheet (inside the same card below the banner, max 45% of screen height, scrolling): variant card, level target, contributions, conductors (two cells), rewards (28 dp emotes with 9 dp locks), unlocked so far, piñata card, "Coming up" expansion, record line, "More Info". Collapsed pill / video pill: 32 dp translucent black, radius 16, ≥48 dp touch target. End strip: banner height, 3 dp draining bar, thanks then three emotes at 14.9 s.

Celebration overlay: a square of `shortestSide × intensity.scale` (0.55–1.1) aligned centre-left/right, bottom or top depending on area and orientation, filled by one particle painter.

## Behaviour and state

**Community highlights store** (full detail in the store section below):
- Subscribes Hermes/PubSub topics `polls.<id>`, `predictions-channel-v1.<id>`, `pinned-chat-updates-v1.<id>`, `raid.<id>`; fetches poll, predictions and pinned via anonymous GQL at start; no raid snapshot exists.
- Reducers make everything monotonic: polls never go back in status or votes; predictions ignore older timestamps and backward statuses; pins are replaced/removed by id, an update triggers a refetch; raids: cancel before leaving = "called off" (final, 3 s), cancel after leaving = removed, repeated "go" ignored.
- Coalescing: urgent changes (new/removed highlight, status change, any pin change, gift bomb, incoming raid, raid gone/cancelled) commit immediately; vote/point/viewer ticks commit at most every 500 ms.
- Deadlines in Twitch time (ServerClock median offset): finished poll +60 s; resolved prediction +60 s; canceled +30 s; pin at `endsAt`; raid cancelled +3 s, gone +60 s, never-left raid at min(goAt, firstSeen+forceRaidNowSeconds) + 90 s; gift bomb 10–30 s by size; incoming raid 6.5 s. "First seen" times never move a removal later.
- Stack rules (Twitch web recency stack): same id updates in place (keeps expanded); one per kind; sticky (pin present at load) appended; otherwise a new item takes the visible slot, unless the visible one is expanded (then it waits right behind as "unseen" → "New …" pill); gift bombs and incoming raids always take the slot. Dismiss: session-only, except pinned messages (persisted list of the last 50 ids). New items arrive expanded only on large screens (`expandNew`, shortest side ≥600 dp) and never for pinned, raid or short-lived kinds.
- GQL hygiene: one fetch per kind at a time, ≥2 s apart with a trailing retry, results discarded if a realtime event of that kind arrived meanwhile, network/timeouts never count as failures, three other failures turn that snapshot off for the session (telemetry logged), a resubscribe re-enables it. While a topic is unavailable, poll GQL every 30 s (paused in background; immediate catch-up on resume).
- Raid flow: `onGoAlong` fires once when a raid seen counting down leaves and the viewer did not choose to stay; the channel screen then runs a `RaidDeparture` (switch after 1350 ms, or 700 ms reduced) showing the "Heading to" overlay; the switch is skipped if the screen is no longer on top/visible/in PiP; a raid cancelled meanwhile calls it off. Arriving on the target shows the overlay fading out (150 ms hold + 300 ms).
- Viewer actions: vote (once per poll, remembered; refused → still votable); stay (optimistic, reverted on failure) / watch again (re-join); unpin (moderators only; refetch after success). Outcomes other than done show a snackbar with "Retry" for failures where offered.

**Hype train store**: phases `hidden → approaching → ongoing → ending/expired → rewards → hidden`; the event queue (cap 100, coalescing bursts ≥5, conductor de-duplication, merge-level suppression); the pure reducer (duplicate/stale/level-mismatch/deferred next-level rules); the sequencer producing timed steps; a presenter count so sequences finish instantly when no banner is mounted; deferral of next-level progressions until their level-up (3 s timeout → GQL); expiry fallback (D5) closing a train 10 s after `expiresAt` (completed from level 2, else expired) unless a queued event extends it; snapshot reconciliation (lagging snapshots ignored, unchanged ones only refresh details, others replace state without animation); approaching banner shown only if its first deadline is >20 s away, closed 5 s after the last deadline; polling every 20 s while realtime is degraded (D12). Style priority: record approaching > record reached > golden Kappa > mythic > shared > treasure > community > regular.

**Celebrations**: the channel events store (outside) exposes the latest `Celebration`; the overlay plays it once (keyed by event id) unless celebrations are off or Subtle (then it is cleared after the frame; in Subtle the subtle feed posted a chat line first). A celebration ends early when its ticker is muted (another route on top) or the app goes to paused/hidden/detached, calling `clearCelebration` once.

## Animations and motion

Global rules: there are TWO reduce-motion sources on Android. `reduceMotionOf(context)` = `MediaQuery.disableAnimations` OR `platformDispatcher.accessibilityFeatures.reduceMotion`; it is used by the gift bomb pin, raid card (`_Smooth`, `_FadeUp`, hit), incoming raid pin, heading overlay, ONSET rocket flicker, celebration overlay, `HighlightCardShell`/`HighlightBar`/`_PollMark` (via `motionDuration`) and the New pill. The hype train reads ONLY `MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf(context)`: `HypeTrainSlot` and `HypeTrainVideoPill` pass it to `store.updatePresentation(reduceMotion:)`, so everything keyed on `store.reduceMotion` (sequencer `_still`, banner fades and announcement switcher, chevron, 200 ms fill, palette morph, level-complete flash, level-6 sheen, rainbow, marquee, every effect, approaching side mark, end-strip swap) ignores the platform accessibility flag. `HighlightSlot`'s 200 ms card switcher also reads only `MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf`. (See Known Android defects: the PC honours both sources everywhere.) `motionDuration(normal)` returns 150 ms (`OnsetMotion.reducedFade`) under reduce motion. Curves: `OnsetMotion.standard = Cubic(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`, `emphasizedDecelerate = Cubic(0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1)`, Flutter `Curves.ease = Cubic(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)`, `easeOut = Cubic(0, 0, 0.58, 1)`, `easeIn = Cubic(0.42, 0, 1, 1)`, `easeInOut = Cubic(0.42, 0, 0.58, 1)`, `fastOutSlowIn = Cubic(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`, `easeOutCubic = Cubic(0.215, 0.61, 0.355, 1)`, `easeOutBack = Cubic(0.175, 0.885, 0.32, 1.275)`, `decelerate(t) = 1 − (1 − t)²`, `elasticOut` (period 0.4) and `bounceOut` (formulas in the particle and trophy sections).

## Strings

Every user-visible string goes through the ARB files (`lib/l10n/app_en.arb`, `lib/l10n/app_fr.arb`); ICU plural/select syntax must be preserved (select keys are enum `.name`s: `HighlightKind` names, `HtVariant` names — regular, goldenKappa, treasure, community, mythic —, `CelebrationEffect` names, `ViewerActionOutcome` names, `PinataEligibility` yes/no). Inline-bold names use `l10nMark(n)` placeholders split by `l10nSpans` (raid "X is raiding **Y**", incoming raid, conductor lines, gift-bomb progress with a rolling number). French uses narrow no-break spaces before `%`, `:`, `!`, `?` as written in the ARB. Hard-coded non-localized strings: `'×$count'` chip, `'${index+1} / $count'` pager, `'%'` on the coin, `'?'` particle glyph, `'1:'` ratio prefix and `'—'` for no ratio, `'Hype Train'`… `HtPalette.a11yName` names (English, not shown on screen), the celebration debug logs.

## Network and data

All reads are anonymous (public Twitch web client id, no user token); viewer actions go through other services owned by the chat/viewer-actions leaves.

Realtime (Hermes WebSocket via `TwitchRealtimeClient`, PubSub-style topics; each `RealtimeMessage` carries a `payload` JSON string and an optional `serverTime` that feeds `ServerClock.observe`; `RealtimeSubscribed(isResubscribe)` and `RealtimeUnavailable` drive degraded/polling modes):
| Topic | Consumer | Message types (field `type`) | Data used |
|---|---|---|---|
| `polls.<channelId>` | highlights | `POLL_CREATE`, `POLL_UPDATE`, `POLL_COMPLETE`, `POLL_TERMINATE`, `POLL_ARCHIVE` | `data.poll` (id, channel id, title, status, choices with id/title/votes, total votes, started/ends/ended times) |
| `predictions-channel-v1.<channelId>` | highlights | event updates | `data.event` (id, channel id, title, status ACTIVE/LOCKED/RESOLVE_PENDING/CANCEL_PENDING/RESOLVED/CANCELED, outcomes with id/title/colour BLUE/PINK/points/users/top predictors, locks/ended times, winning outcome id), `data.timestamp` |
| `pinned-chat-updates-v1.<channelId>` | highlights | `pin-message`, `update-message`, `unpin-message` | pinned message (id, message id, sender id/login/display name/colour/badges, text and fragments with emote ids, pinned by, starts/ends at); update/unpin carry only `id` (≤64 chars) |
| `raid.<channelId>` | highlights | `raid_update_v2`/`raid_update`, `raid_go_v2`/`raid_go`, `raid_cancel_v2`/`raid_cancel` | `raid` node: id, source id, target id/login/display name/profile image, viewer count, `force_raid_now_seconds`, `raid_created_at` (→ goAt) |
| `hype-train-events-v2.<channelId>` | hype train | `hype-train-approaching`, `hype-train-start`, `hype-train-shared-conversion`, `hype-train-progression`, `hype-train-level-up`, `hype-train-conductor-update`, `hype-train-end`, `hype-train-shared-merge-trains`, `hype-train-shared-channel-left`; ignored: `hype-train-cooldown-expiration`, `hype-train-reward-eligibility`, `hype-train-rewards` | approaching (id, goal, `events_remaining_durations{"n": seconds}`, participants, level one rewards, variant); train execution; progression (train id, sequence id = running total, user, action e.g. CHEER / TIER_n_SUB / TIER_n_GIFTED_SUB, source BITS/SUBS…, quantity, is large, progress {level{value, goal, rewards}, goal, value, total, remaining seconds, all-time-high state}, expiry, shared per-channel progress, treasure); level-up (time to expire, progress, full train in GQL shape); conductor (source, user, participations); end (reason COMPLETED/EXPIRED…, train id, ended at, participation totals, rewards); merge (train, previous progress, previous/joining channels); channel left (train id, continues in user, `shouldTrainEnd`) |
| `celebration-events-v1.<channelId>` (channel events store, outside) | celebrations | `alert_sets_celebration` | `type` (FIREWORKS/RAIN/FLOAT/FLAMETHROWER/EXTINCTION_EVENT; others dropped), `area`, `intensity`, `duration` seconds (default 5, clamped 0.5–15 s), `alert_event_id`, `celebration_source` (`system_gifting` = automatic), `primary_color_hex` |

GQL (anonymous POST `gql.twitch.tv/gql`, query documents, services outside this area): `GetViewablePoll(channelID)` → poll or null; `GetActivePrediction(channelID, resolvedCount = 1)` → active, locked and last resolved prediction; `GetPinnedChat(channelID)` → current pin; `GetHypeTrainExecution(channelId)` → `HtOngoingSnapshot(train)` / `HtApproachingSnapshot(approaching)` / `HtNoTrain`; `GetHypeTrainRewards(channelId)` → rewards by level ("Coming up"); `GetOngoingPinata(channelID)` → `PinataTrainDetails` stage and eligibility; `HypeTrainStatuses(channelIds)` → `HtCardStatus` for followed-stream cards (`HypeTrainCardPill`). Snapshot cadence: highlights at start, on resubscribe, on app resume (degraded kinds), every 30 s while a topic is down, refetch on pin update/unpin success, minimum 2 s between fetches of a kind; hype train at start, on resubscribe, on resume, on missed start/level gap/stale train id/deferred timeout/approaching expiry, every 20 s while realtime is degraded, minimum 2 s apart.

Images: emotes from `https://static-cdn.jtvnw.net/emoticons/v2/<id>/default/dark/3.0` (id must match `^[A-Za-z0-9_]{1,80}$`); badges and avatars only from https Twitch CDN hosts (`static-cdn.jtvnw.net`; `twitchImageOrNull` for raid avatars), otherwise the initial-letter fallback; golden Kappa emote id `80393`. Local assets: `assets/celebrations/rocket.svg`, `gift_closed.png`, `gift_open.png` (512 px), `spark_lime.png`, `spark_mint.png`, `spark_white.png`, `spark_dot.png` (128 px). Links opened: `https://www.twitch.tv/popout/<login>/chat` (take part in polls/predictions, in-app web page), the channel report URL (`twitchReportUri`), `https://help.twitch.tv/s/article/hype-train-guide`. Telemetry: `community highlights: <topic prefix> snapshot off: <error>` after three failures.

## Wide and tablet layout

- Large screen = shortest side ≥ 600 dp (`isLargeScreenSize`). There, new polls and predictions arrive **expanded** (`expandNew`), never the pinned message, raid or short-lived pins; the landscape keyboard does not hide the hype train (`largeScreen` in placement); the hype train banner is capped at 560 dp wide and the full banner needs ≥480 dp of chat width (else compact) — caps applied by the channel screen.
- Bottom sheets (highlight details) are constrained to 480 dp wide and 80% of the screen height; the hype train details open inside the banner card (max 45% of screen height, `detailsMaxHeight` override) in portrait/wide chat, as a sheet in compact layouts.
- The gift bomb pin uses its wide two-line layout whenever its column is ≥300 dp, else one line; the raid card drops its track in compact; text scaling is clamped (1.3 for cards/pins/pills, 2.0 for full hype train banner and strip) and the hype train banner grows up to 2× height with text.
- Landscape: chat side column/overlay → compact slots; hidden chat → video pills; celebrations align to the chat side (setting `landscapeChatLeftSide`) for CHAT area and to the player side for VIDEO area.
- RTL is supported throughout (fills grow from the right, marquees run left→right, the rocket launch flips, split-colour text mirrors).

## PC equivalent

Windows desktop (Rust + Slint; macOS/Linux later). Nothing is dropped:
- Stores become Rust state machines (one per open channel tab/window) fed by the shared realtime client and GQL client, with the exact reducers, queue, sequencer, timers and deadlines above; use a monotonic clock plus the `ServerClock` median offset for Twitch time, and a timer wheel/tokio for coalescing (500 ms), deadlines, refetch gaps (2 s), polling (30 s / 20 s), step timers and 1 Hz countdowns. Expose them to Slint as models/properties; Slint `animate` blocks or per-frame timers implement every tween with the listed curves (`cubic-bezier(...)` easing in Slint, custom for elastic/bounce/decelerate).
- Layout mapping: the chat column is a resizable side panel; the hype train slot and highlight slot sit at its top. "Compact" applies when the chat panel is narrower than the phone thresholds (300 dp for gift bombs, 480 dp for the full hype banner) or when chat is undocked over the video; "hidden chat" (theatre/fullscreen with chat closed) shows the video pills at the top-left of the player. Bottom sheets become anchored popovers or a modal panel 480 px wide. PiP becomes the always-on-top mini player: raid overlays are never drawn there, and the raid switch is skipped while the window is minimized, in mini-player mode, or another modal page covers the channel (`canLeave`).
- Input: taps → clicks; swipe-up-to-collapse on the hype banner → a collapse button plus mouse wheel up / drag up; hover states on all buttons; keyboard: Tab/Enter/Space on cards (expand), arrows on the pager, Escape closes sheets; screen-reader labels map to UIA names/live regions (Slint accessible-role/label; live-region announcements via the platform accessibility bridge).
- Rendering: painters (rings, dotted track, gift ring, particles via instanced textured quads with modulate tint, speedlines, coin, sweep) are drawn with a 2D vector/GPU layer (e.g. femtovg/skia in Slint `Path`/custom renderer or wgpu underlay); the split-colour text is rendered twice with complementary clip rectangles at the fill edge; shader masks (mythic shimmer) become a gradient alpha mask; the rocket SVG is rendered with resvg into body/flame textures, flame transformed per frame. Assets in `assets/celebrations/` are bundled unchanged; emote/badge/avatar images go through the app's shared image cache, decoded at displayed pixel size × scale factor. (Renderer superseded by D3: Slint with Skia.)
- Reduce motion follows Windows "Show animations in Windows" (SPI_GETCLIENTAREAANIMATION) / macOS "Reduce motion" / GNOME `enable-animations`, with an in-app override, applying the same reduced variants; the gift-bomb drain bar keeps real time regardless.
- Lifecycle: "app background" = window minimized or occluded → highlights/hype train polling pause and resume with an immediate catch-up; celebrations end when the window is minimized or the channel view is hidden.
- Known Android defects (real bugs in the Flutter app; the PC fixes them, keeping the intended behaviour):
  1. Reduce motion ignored by the hype train and the highlight slot: `HypeTrainSlot` and `HypeTrainVideoPill` feed `store.updatePresentation(reduceMotion: MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf(context))`, and `HighlightSlot`'s card AnimatedSwitcher uses `MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf`. With only the platform accessibility reduce-motion flag on (`platformDispatcher.accessibilityFeatures.reduceMotion`), Android still plays the full hype-train sequences (marquees, rainbows, coin/rocket/trophy/mythic effects, flash, sheen, fades) and the 200 ms card fade, while every `reduceMotionOf` widget in the same screen goes reduced. PC fix: one reduce-motion signal = OS setting OR in-app override, honoured by every widget in this area, including the hype train store's presentation flag and the highlight slot switcher.
  2. Marquee emotes: `HypeTrainMarquee` does `emotes.take(6)` before dropping emotes without a URL, so a list whose first entries lack URLs shows fewer than 6 emotes even when later ones are valid. PC fix: filter URL-less emotes first, then take up to 6 (and measure the width from that list).
  3. Physical (non-directional) layout in RTL. ONSET ships only EN and FR (both LTR), so nothing is visibly wrong today, but these spots would break in an RTL language: (a) the New pill's gap in `HighlightPager` is `EdgeInsets.only(left: 6)` (in RTL the gap ends up between the pill and the edge instead of beside the arrow); (b) `HighlightCardShell` header row padding is `EdgeInsets.only(left: 12)` (in RTL the 26 dp tile sits flush against the card's right edge with 0 inset and the 12 dp moves to the left of the close button; every poll, prediction and pinned card); (c) the prediction meta `Wrap` in `_OutcomeRow` has `EdgeInsets.only(left: 2)`; (d) the raid card track (raid_card.dart `_Track`) is physical: source avatar `Positioned(left: 0)`, target avatar and hit `right: 0`, progress fill from `left: 0`, rocket at `left: max(0, W − 24)·value` with a fixed angle π/4 — so in RTL the raid still travels left → right (source on the left, target on the right) while the header text reads right → left, unlike `RocketLaunch`, which flips; (e) the incoming raid pin places the avatar at `left: 0` and the rocket at a physical `right: −4, top: −2`. PC fix: use start/end (directional) insets and positions everywhere, and mirror the raid track, its fill and the rocket's travel (flip x, angle mirrored) in RTL, so the layout stays correct if an RTL language is ever added; in LTR the result is identical to Android.
  4. `HighlightCountdown` never restarts: its `Timer.periodic(1 s)` is created once in `initState` and cancels for good when the deadline passes; there is no `didUpdateWidget`, so a later deadline on the same widget (raid `goAt` arriving or moving, a re-timed poll/prediction) stops ticking every second and updates only when the parent rebuilds. PC fix: restart the 1 Hz timer whenever the deadline changes (and while the deadline is in the future).
- Determinism: port Dart's `math.Random` and `String.hashCode` if pixel-identical particle placement is required (tests compare by seed); otherwise use a seeded PRNG with the same draw order — the visuals and test properties (bounded count ≤120, same seed → same frames) are what must be preserved.
## Per-file reference: community highlights

### lib/screens/channel/community_highlights/pinned_notice.dart

Single top-level function `String pinnedNotice(PinnedChatMessage pin, AppLocalizations l)`. It is the one-line notice text that chat (outside this area) inserts when a message gets pinned. Logic: take `pin.pinnedBy`, `trim()` it; if the trimmed value is non-null AND non-empty return `l.chatPinnedBy(name: by)` (EN "{name} pinned a message" style, see Strings), otherwise (null, empty or whitespace-only moderator name) return `l.chatPinnedNew` (EN "New pinned message" style). No other branching, no formatting of the name (the trimmed string is passed verbatim, so a name with inner spaces keeps them). Rust port: `fn pinned_notice(pin: &PinnedChatMessage, l: &L10n) -> String` with `pin.pinned_by.as_deref().map(str::trim).filter(|s| !s.is_empty())`.

### lib/screens/channel/community_highlights/raid_text.dart

Two helpers shared by the raid card, the "Heading to" overlay and the incoming raid pin. `int raidViewerCount(int count) => math.max(0, count)` — clamps negative viewer counts (Twitch sometimes sends -1 or garbage) to 0; this clamped value is both the number displayed and the value that selects the ICU plural form. `String raidViewers(int count, AppLocalizations l) => l.raidViewers(count: raidViewerCount(count))` — the localized "1,204 viewers" string, in the given locale's grouping (EN comma, FR narrow no-break space via ICU number formatting of the ARB `{count, plural, ...}` message). Port: identical clamp before plural selection; plural must use the clamped value (0 picks the "other"/"zero" branch as the ARB defines).

### lib/screens/channel/community_highlights/gift_bomb_feed.dart

`ReactionDisposer feedGiftBombs({required GiftBomb? Function() latest, required void Function(GiftBomb bomb) show})` — a MobX `reaction<GiftBomb?>` whose tracking function is `latest()` and whose effect calls `show(bomb)` whenever the tracked value changes AND is non-null. Because it is a `reaction` (not `autorun`) the effect does NOT fire for the value present when the reaction is created: a bomb announced before the channel screen began feeding is never pinned late. `latest` reads the chat store's "latest banner-worthy gift bomb" observable, which chat only sets after its chat-delay (the user's chat delay setting used to sync chat with video), so the banner appears in step with the video, not with the raw IRC arrival. A reaction fires on identity change: the same `GiftBomb` object set twice does not re-show; a new object (even with equal fields) does. Setting it to null does nothing. The returned disposer must be called on screen dispose. Port: an observer on the chat's latest-bomb signal with "skip initial value" semantics and change detection by identity (e.g. a monotonically increasing bomb sequence id).

### lib/screens/channel/community_highlights/pinned_to_irc.dart

`IRCMessage pinnedToIrcMessage(PinnedChatMessage pin, {required String channelId, required String channelLogin, String? viewerLogin})` converts a GQL/realtime pinned message into the same `IRCMessage` a live PRIVMSG would produce, so the pinned card renders it with the chat renderer (badges, name colour, Twitch emotes, 7TV/BTTV/FFZ third-party words). It delegates to `buildPrivmsgIrcMessage` (lib/utils/irc_privmsg_builder.dart, outside this area) with:
- `fragments`: if `pin.fragments` is empty, a single fragment `(text: pin.text, emoteId: null)`; otherwise each fragment mapped to `(text: f.text, emoteId: f.emoteId)` in order (a fragment with an emote id becomes a Twitch emote range in the synthesized `emotes` tag).
- `login: pin.senderLogin`, `displayName: pin.senderDisplayName`, `userId: pin.senderId`, `color: pin.color` (hex or null).
- `badges`: each `pin.badges` entry as `(setId: badge.setId, version: badge.version)`, order preserved.
- `id: 'pin-${pin.messageId ?? pin.id}'` — prefixed so it never collides with the real chat message id (the original message id when known, else the pin id).
- `channelId`, `channelLogin`, `viewerLogin` passed through (viewerLogin lets the renderer highlight mentions of the viewer).
- `sentAt: pin.startsAt ?? DateTime.now().toUtc()` — the pin start time, else now in UTC.

### lib/screens/channel/community_highlights/highlight_math.dart

Pure math for poll and prediction cards.

`List<int> percentages(List<int> counts)` — whole percentages summing to exactly 100 by the largest-remainder (Hamilton) method. Steps: (1) negative counts are treated as 0; `total = Σ max(c,0)`. (2) If total == 0 return a list of zeros of the same length. (3) `exact[i] = max(c_i,0) * 100 / total` (double). (4) `floors[i] = floor(exact[i])`. (5) `left = 100 − Σ floors`. (6) Order indexes by descending remainder `exact[i] − floors[i]`; ties broken by ascending index (earlier choice first). (7) Walk that order adding 1 to `floors[i]` and decrementing `left` until `left == 0`. Example: [1,1,1] → [34,33,33]; [2,1] → [67,33]; [0,0] → [0,0].

`Set<int> leaders(List<int> counts)` — empty set for an empty list; `top = max(counts)`; if `top <= 0` empty set; else every index whose count equals top (ties give several leaders). Used to bold/mark the winning poll bars.

`double? returnRatio(Prediction prediction, PredictionOutcome outcome)` — null if `outcome.points <= 0` (nobody predicted it), else `prediction.totalPoints / outcome.points` (double division).

`String formatRatio(double? ratio)` — "—" (U+2014 em dash) when ratio is null or not finite. Otherwise `ratio.toStringAsFixed(2)`, strip trailing zeros with regex `0+$`, then strip a trailing `.` with `\.$`, then pass through `localDecimal(text)` (lib/l10n/l10n.dart: replaces the `.` by the current locale's decimal separator, so French shows `1:1,02`), and prefix `1:`. Examples: 2.4300 → "1:2.43"; 2.5 → "1:2.5"; 3.0 → "1:3"; 1.0249 → "1:1.02"; FR 1.02 → "1:1,02". Note `toStringAsFixed` rounding is half-away-from-zero on the binary double (Dart semantics) — the Rust port must reproduce `format!("{:.2}")` carefully (Rust rounds half-to-even on the decimal representation of the binary value; both operate on the exact binary value, so results match except for exact binary ties, which cannot occur for 2-decimal rounding of ratios except values like x.125 — use a Dart-compatible formatter to be exact).

### lib/screens/channel/community_highlights/highlight_reducers.dart

Pure reducers that make realtime + GQL updates monotonic.

Poll rank (`_pollRank`): active=0; completed or terminated=1; archived=2; unknown=−1.
`Poll? reducePoll(Poll? current, PollEvent event)`:
1. If `event.kind == PollEventKind.archive` OR `event.poll.status == archived`: return null if current is null or has the same id as the event's poll; otherwise keep current (an archive of another poll doesn't remove ours).
2. If the event poll status is unknown → return current unchanged.
3. If current is null or has a different id → return the new poll (a new poll replaces the old).
4. Same id: if the new rank < current rank → keep current (a late "update" after completion never moves it back). If same rank and `next.totalVotes < current.totalVotes` → keep current (votes never go down within a status). Else return next.

Prediction rank (`_predictionRank`): active=0; locked=1; resolvePending or cancelPending=2; resolved or canceled=3; unknown=−1.
`({Prediction? prediction, DateTime? timestamp}) reducePrediction(Prediction? current, PredictionEvent event, {DateTime? lastTimestamp})`:
1. Unknown status → `(current, lastTimestamp)` unchanged.
2. Current null or different id → `(next, event.timestamp)`.
3. Same id: if `event.timestamp` and `lastTimestamp` both non-null and the event is strictly before lastTimestamp → unchanged (older updates ignored).
4. If new rank < current rank → unchanged.
5. Else `(next, event.timestamp ?? lastTimestamp)`.

`({PinnedChatMessage? pin, bool refetch}) reducePinned(PinnedChatMessage? current, ChEvent event)` (pattern match):
- `PinEvent(message)` → `(message, false)` — any pin replaces.
- `UnpinEvent(id)` → `(current?.id == id ? null : current, false)`.
- `PinUpdateEvent(id)` → `(current, current?.id == id)` — an update carries no details, so a refetch is requested only if it concerns the current pin.
- anything else → `(current, false)`.

`bool pinExpired(PinnedChatMessage pin, DateTime now)` — true iff `pin.endsAt` is non-null and `now >= endsAt` (Twitch sends nothing when a timed pin ends).

`class RaidState { final OutgoingRaid raid; final bool gone; final bool cancelled; }` — const constructor, `gone` and `cancelled` default false. `cancelled` = called off before it left: shows "cancelled" briefly (kRaidCancelledLinger) then goes, never takes the viewer.

`RaidState? reduceRaid(RaidState? current, RaidEvent event, {required String channelId})`:
1. `event.raid.sourceId != channelId` → current (another channel's raid ignored).
2. `same = current != null && current.raid.id == raid.id`. If same and `current.cancelled` → current (cancel is final; later events of that raid change nothing).
3. Switch on kind: `cancel` → if not same: current; else if `current.gone` → null (cancel after it left removes it); else `RaidState(current.raid, cancelled: true)` (keeps the OLD raid data). `go` → if same and already gone → current (identical object; repeated go events change nothing); else `RaidState(raid, gone: true)`. `update` → `RaidState(raid, gone: same && current.gone)` (fresh data, keeps gone).
Tested by test/screens/channel/community_highlights/highlight_reducers_test.dart.

### lib/screens/channel/community_highlights/highlight_stack.dart

Enum `HighlightKind { poll, prediction, pinned, raid, giftBomb, incomingRaid }` (this declaration order is the order of `HighlightKind.values`; `.name` strings are "poll", "prediction", "pinned", "raid", "giftBomb", "incomingRaid" and are used as ICU select keys in ARB messages and in stack keys). Extension `shortLived` is true for `giftBomb` and `incomingRaid`: they take the slot at once, never arrive expanded, and show even under the hype train's details.

`class HighlightItem { kind, id, sticky=false, expanded=false; copyWith({bool? expanded}) }` — `sticky` = known before the screen opened (an existing pinned message at load), appended at the end instead of grabbing attention.

`class HighlightStack` — immutable; fields `items` (List, default empty), `currentIndex` (default 0), `unseenKey` (String? `"kind.name:id"`), `dismissed` (Set<String> of keys closed this session). Key format `'${kind.name}:$id'`.
- `current` → null if empty, else `items[currentIndex.clamp(0, len−1)]`.
- `unseen` → the item whose key equals `unseenKey`, unless not found or it is the current one (then null). `unseenBehind` = `unseen != null`.
- `_copy({items, currentIndex, unseenKey, dismissed})`: clamps index to `[0, max(len−1,0)]`; keeps the unseen key only if that item still exists and is not at the (new) current index (seen once shown, gone with its highlight). NOTE: passing `unseenKey: null` cannot clear it (null-coalescing); clearing happens only via the existence/current check.
- `upsert(item)`: (1) dismissed key → unchanged. (2) If an item of the same kind exists with the same id → replace in place with `item.copyWith(expanded: old.expanded)` (keeps expanded state; sticky/other fields from new item). (3) Else if the kind exists with another id → `remove(kind)` first. (4) If `item.sticky` or the list is empty → append at end; currentIndex = 0 if the stack was empty, else unchanged. (5) Else if the current item is expanded AND the new kind is not shortLived → insert at `currentIndex + 1` and set `unseenKey` to the new item's key (the "New" pill). (6) Else insert AT `currentIndex` (it becomes the visible one; the previously visible shifts right).
- `remove(kind)`: unchanged if absent; removes it; new index = `currentIndex − 1` if the removed index was before current, else unchanged (then clamped).
- `dismiss(kind, id)`: removes the kind if an item with that kind AND id exists, then adds the key to `dismissed` (even if absent — pre-emptive dismissal).
- `select(index)`: no-op on empty; clamps; selecting the unseen item clears the pill via `_copy`.
- `toggleExpanded()`: flips `expanded` of the current item.
Tested by test/screens/channel/community_highlights/highlight_stack_test.dart.

### lib/screens/channel/community_highlights/stores/community_highlights_store.dart

MobX store (`CommunityHighlightsStore = CommunityHighlightsStoreBase with _$CommunityHighlightsStore`; the `.g.dart` mixin is generated, do not port literally — port the observable semantics). One per channel screen. Holds the channel's poll, prediction, pinned chat message, outgoing raid, gift-bomb banner, incoming-raid banner, and the `HighlightStack` deciding which card shows above chat.

Typedefs: `PollVoter = Future<ViewerActionOutcome> Function({required String pollId, required String choiceId})`; `PinRemover = Future<ViewerActionOutcome> Function(String pinId)`; `RaidMembership = Future<ViewerActionOutcome> Function(String raidId, {required bool join})`; `PollVote = ({String pollId, String choiceId})`.

Constructor params: `channelId` (required), `channelLogin?`, `channelName?`, `service: TwitchCommunityHighlightsService` (anonymous GQL), `realtime: TwitchRealtimeClient` (Hermes/PubSub), `serverClock?` (default new `ServerClock()`), `expandNew = false` (large screens: new polls/predictions arrive expanded), `onGoAlong?`, `isPinHidden?`, `onHidePin?`, `pollVoter?`, `raidMembership?`, `pinRemover?`, `canRemovePins?`.
Construction sequence: for each `HighlightTopic.values` (polls → `polls.<id>`, predictions → `predictions-channel-v1.<id>`, pinned → `pinned-chat-updates-v1.<id>`, raid → `raid.<id>`): `realtime.subscribe(topic.topicFor(channelId))`, keep the subscription, listen to `events` → `_onTopicEvent(topic, e)`; if subscribe throws, log and add the topic to `_degraded`. Then fire `_fetchPoll()`, `_fetchPredictions()`, `_fetchPinned(initial: true)` (no fetch for raid: there is no raid snapshot) and `_updatePolling()`.

Observables (`@readonly`, i.e. public getter, private setter): `poll`, `prediction`, `pinned`, `raid` (RaidState), `giftBomb` (GiftBombBanner), `incomingRaid` (IncomingRaidBanner), `stayingFor` (raid id), `departingFor` (raid id), `pollVote` (PollVote), `voting` (bool), `stack` (HighlightStack, initial empty).
Computeds: `stayingOnRaid` = raid != null && raid.raid.id == stayingFor; `departing` = raid != null && raid.raid.id == departingFor; `votedChoiceId` = pollVote.choiceId if `pollVote.pollId == poll?.id` else null; `canVote` = pollVoter != null && poll != null && poll.isActive && votedChoiceId == null; `current` = stack.current; `visible` = stack.items non-empty.
Other getters: `canUnpin` = pinRemover != null && pinned != null && (canRemovePins?.call() ?? false); `newPins` broadcast stream of `PinnedChatMessage` (each new pin that arrives by realtime PinEvent with a different id from the current and not hidden by the viewer; chat uses it to insert the "pinned" notice); `serverNow()` = `serverClock.now()` (Twitch time learnt from notification timestamps).

Viewer actions:
- `unpin()` (async): returns null if already unpinning (`_unpinning` re-entrancy flag), no remover, no pin, or `!canUnpin`; else sets the flag, awaits `remover(pin.id)`; on `done` triggers `_fetchPinned()`; returns the outcome; flag cleared in finally.
- `markDeparting(OutgoingRaid? raid)` action: `departingFor = raid?.id`.
- `stayOnRaid()` action: raid = `_nextRaid ?? _raid`; null → null. Sets `stayingFor = id` immediately (optimistic), awaits `raidMembership?.call(id, join: false)`; if the outcome is non-null, not done, and stayingFor is still that id → revert `stayingFor = null` (runInAction). Returns outcome.
- `joinRaidAgain()` action: raid = `_nextRaid ?? _raid`; returns null unless raid exists, its id == stayingFor, not gone, not cancelled. Clears stayingFor and returns `raidMembership?.call(id, join: true)` (no revert on failure).
- `voteInPoll(choiceId)` action: null if voting, no voter, no poll, `!canVote`, or choiceId not among `poll.choices`. Sets voting=true; awaits voter; on done sets `pollVote = (pollId, choiceId)`; finally voting=false. Returns outcome.
- `select(index)`, `toggleExpanded()` delegate to the stack.
- `dismissCurrent()`: if current is a pinned item, `onHidePin?.call(id)` (pins stay hidden forever via persisted ids, see Settings); then `stack.dismiss(kind, id)` (session-only for other kinds).
- `showGiftBomb(GiftBomb bomb, {ValueListenable<GiftBomb>? live})` action: `duration = bannerDuration(bomb.count)` (from chat/gift_bomb_tracker.dart: <5 → null (ignored), <25 → 10 s, <50 → 15 s, <75 → 20 s, <100 → 25 s, else 30 s); ignored if disposed or null. `startedAt = serverNow()`, `_nextGiftBomb = GiftBombBanner(bomb, startedAt, endsAt: startedAt+duration, live)`, `_changed(urgent: true)`. A newer bomb replaces the one up.
- `showIncomingRaid(IncomingRaid raid)` action: ignored if disposed. `now = serverNow()`; prune `_incomingPinned` entries older than or equal to 2 min; key = `raid.raiderKey` (= `(raiderLogin ?? raiderName).toLowerCase()`); if the key is present → return (same raid heard twice — its USERNOTICE and the raid the viewer followed here). Else record `now`, `_nextIncomingRaid = IncomingRaidBanner(raid, id: 'raid-${++_incomingCount}', endsAt: now + 6.5 s)`, urgent change.

Realtime handling `_onTopicEvent(topic, event)` (ignored after dispose):
- `RealtimeMessage(payload, serverTime)`: if serverTime non-null `serverClock.observe(serverTime)`; `_apply(ChEvent.parse(topic, payload))`.
- `RealtimeSubscribed(isResubscribe)`: if resubscribe (new connection) → remove topic from `_gqlOff` and `_failures` (snapshot gets another chance); remove from `_degraded`; `_updatePolling()`; if resubscribe → `_fetch(topic)` to catch up.
- `RealtimeUnavailable()`: add to `_degraded`, `_updatePolling()`.

`_apply(ChEvent)`:
- `PollEvent`: ignore if `poll.channelId` non-null and != channelId. `_saw(polls)` (increments per-topic event counter used to discard stale GQL answers). `_nextPoll = reducePoll(before, event)`; `_changed(urgent: _isUrgent(before, next))`.
- `PredictionEvent`: same channel check; `_saw(predictions)`; reducePrediction with `_predictionTimestamp`; store both; urgent per `_isUrgent`.
- `PinEvent | UnpinEvent | PinUpdateEvent`: `_saw(pinned)` except for PinUpdateEvent (its refetch is newer). `next = reducePinned(_nextPinned, event)`; if refetch → `_fetchPinned()`. If PinEvent with a pin whose id differs from `_nextPinned?.id` and not hidden → `_newPins.add(pin)`. If `next.pin` is not identical to `_nextPinned` → set it, `_pinnedSticky = false`, urgent change.
- `RaidEvent`: ignore if the raid id is in `_raidsCalledOff`. `_nextRaid = reduceRaid(before, event, channelId)`; return if identical. `_changed(urgent: before == null || next == null || gone changed || cancelled changed)` (viewer-count updates are coalesced). If next is cancelled → add its id to `_raidsCalledOff`, keeping at most 8 (drop the oldest-inserted). Go-along: if before existed, was neither gone nor cancelled, next is gone, same raid id, and that id != stayingFor → `onGoAlong?.call(next.raid)` (only a raid seen counting down moves the viewer; one first seen already gone does not).
- `ChUnknown` → nothing.

`_isUrgent(before, after)`: two Polls → urgent iff id or status differ; two Predictions → same rule; both null → false; any other combination (appeared/disappeared) → true.

Commit coalescing `_changed({urgent})`: if urgent, or never committed, or ≥500 ms since the last commit → cancel pending timer, `_commit()` now. Otherwise arm (once, `??=`) a timer for `500 ms − elapsed` that commits. Uses `clock.now()` (package:clock, fakeable in tests).

`_commit()` action: `_lastCommit = clock.now()`; copy all six `_next*` into the observables; then sync the stack in this fixed order: poll, prediction, pinned (id null if hidden by the viewer; `sticky: _pinnedSticky`), raid (by `raid.raid.id`), giftBomb (`banner.id`), incomingRaid (`banner.id`); then `_scheduleDeadlines()`.
`_sync(stack, kind, id, {sticky})`: id null → `stack.remove(kind)`; same id already present → unchanged; else `upsert(HighlightItem(kind, id, sticky, expanded: expandNew && kind != pinned && kind != raid && !shortLived))` (pinned, raid — whose card has no collapsed form — and short-lived pins never arrive expanded so they never hold a new highlight back).

Deadlines `_scheduleDeadlines()` (all Twitch time, `now = serverNow()`); each `_deadline(kind, at, clear)` cancels the previous timer of that kind, and if `at` is non-null arms `Timer(max(at − serverNow(), 0))` that removes the timer entry, then (if not disposed) runs `clear()` and `_commit()`:
- Poll: if poll null or active → none; else `earliest(poll.endedAt, firstSeenEnded(poll.id)) + 60 s` → `_nextPoll = null`.
- Prediction: linger 60 s if resolved, 30 s if canceled, else none; `earliest(prediction.endedAt, firstSeenEnded) + linger`.
- Pinned: `_nextPinned?.endsAt`.
- Raid: cancelled → `firstSeenCancelled(id) + 3 s`; gone → `firstSeenGone(id) + 60 s` (from the first "go"; Twitch sends it several times); otherwise (counting down) → `earliest(raid.goAt, firstSeen(id) + raid.forceRaidNowSeconds s) + 60 s + 30 s` (a lost go/cancel or an absurd/missing departure time never leaves it up forever).
- GiftBomb: `endsAt`. IncomingRaid: `endsAt`.
`_earliest(a, b)` = a if a non-null and before b, else b. `_FirstSeen` remembers the time an id was first observed and keeps it while the id stays (so later commits never push a removal back): `of(id, now)` resets when the id changes.

GQL `_guarded(topic, fetch)`: returns immediately if disposed or topic is in `_gqlOff`. If a fetch of this topic is in flight → remember this fetch in `_again[topic]` (runs once after the current ends; the running one may predate the change) and return the running future. If the last fetch started <2 s ago → arm a single trailing timer at the end of the gap (`_trailing[topic] ??=`) that re-runs `_guarded`, return. Else record `_lastFetch = clock.now()` and run: on success reset `_failures[topic] = 0`; `api.NetworkException` / `api.TimeoutException` / `dart:async TimeoutException` are logged only (offline is not a broken API, failure count untouched); any other error increments `_failures`; at 3 consecutive failures the topic is put in `_gqlOff` for the session, `_updatePolling()`, and `Telemetry.log('community highlights: ${topic.prefix} snapshot off: $e')` (reported in release too, means Twitch changed the query). Finally: remove in-flight entry, and if an `_again` fetch was queued run it via `_guarded`.
`_fresh(topic, request)`: snapshot `_events[topic]` before the request; after it, return null if disposed or any realtime event of that topic was applied meanwhile (the event is newer than the answer).
- `_fetchPoll`: `service.getPoll(channelId)`; null answer → `_nextPoll = null`; else `reducePoll(_nextPoll, PollEvent(answer.isActive ? update : complete, answer))`; urgent change.
- `_fetchPredictions`: `service.getPredictions(channelId)` returns active/locked/lastResolved; `recent` = lastResolved.endedAt non-null and `serverNow − endedAt <= 2 min`; snapshot = active ?? locked ?? (recent ? resolved : null). If snapshot id equals the current one → reducePrediction (lagging reads never move status back); else replace and reset `_predictionTimestamp = null`. Urgent change.
- `_fetchPinned({initial=false})`: `service.getPinned(channelId)`; drop it if `pinExpired(pin, serverNow())`; `_pinnedSticky = initial` (the pin existing when the screen opened is sticky); urgent change.

Polling `_updatePolling()`: topics = degraded topics except raid and except `_gqlOff`. If disposed, in background, or empty → cancel `_pollTimer`. Else `_pollTimer ??= Timer.periodic(30 s, fetch every (current) degraded non-raid topic)` (note: the periodic callback does not re-check `_gqlOff`; `_guarded` does).
App lifecycle: `handleAppBackground()` → `_inBackground = true`, `_updatePolling()`. `handleAppResume()` → only if it was in background: clear flag, `_updatePolling()`, and fetch immediately every degraded non-raid topic not in `_gqlOff`.
`dispose()`: idempotent; closes `_newPins`, cancels topic listeners and subscriptions, all deadline timers, trailing timers, commit timer, poll timer. Debug logging only in debug builds: `CommunityHighlightsStore(<channelId>) <message>`.

### lib/screens/channel/celebrations/particles/celebration_view.dart

`CelebrationParticles(preset, progress: Animation<double>, duration, seed, reduceMotion=false)` StatefulWidget: one CustomPainter repainting on `progress` — no widget per particle, no ticker of its own (the owner of progress owns timing). Static `_live` counter of celebrations on screen; when the last one disposes, `_Glyphs.clear()` frees cached laid-out "?" glyphs. initState: `_live++`; if sprites not decoded → `CelebrationSprites.load()` then setState when available (so a paused/short celebration doesn't keep stand-ins). `_simulation = CelebrationSimulation(preset, duration, seed, reduceMotion)`, rebuilt in didUpdateWidget if any of those change. `CelebrationPainter(simulation, progress, sprites)` (repaints with `progress`). `paint(canvas, size)`:
1. Return if size empty/non-finite. `frames = simulation.frameAt(progress.value)`; return if empty.
2. `unit = size.shortestSide`; save; clip to the rect.
3. For each frame in order (back to front): `center = (x·width, y·height)`; `extent = size·unit` (skip if <= 0). Image = sprite → `sprites?[f.sprite]`; glow/dot → the glow image; others → null. If image is null → flush the atlas first (preserves stacking).
   - sprite: no image → `_vectorSpark`; else atlas add (extent wide).
   - rect (confetti): save; translate center; rotate `f.rotation`; scale (1, max(|flip|, 0.12)) (never fully edge-on: it flickers rather than vanishing); fill `color @ opacity`; RRect centred `extent × extent·aspect`, corner radius `0.25·extent`; restore.
   - glow: atlas add glow image with width `extent·2` (glow radius = extent).
   - dot (ember): atlas add glow at `extent·2` in f.color, then glow at `extent·0.7` in `lerp(f.color, #F5F5F5, 0.6)` (hot pale core).
   - ring: stroke width `max(stroke·unit, 0.5)`, `color @ opacity`, circle radius extent.
   - arc: stroke, round cap, same width; oval radius extent; draw arc (arcStart, arcSweep) and the opposite arc (arcStart + π, arcSweep); reset cap to butt.
   - glyph: `glyph = _Glyphs.of(color, opacity)`; `scale = extent / 64`; save, translate center, rotate, scale, paint the TextPainter centred (−w/2, −h/2); restore.
4. Flush atlas; restore.
`_vectorSpark`: sprite = f.sprite ?? white; base colour = (sprite is white AND f.color != white) ? f.color : sprite.fallback (lime #C8FF3D, mint #3DDBA6, white #F5F5F5, dot → green #12A87A); fill `base @ opacity`. Dot sprite → filled circle radius extent/2. Otherwise a four-point star: `r = extent/2`, `w = 0.28·r`; save, translate, rotate; path: moveTo(0,−r) quad (0.4w, −0.4w) → (r,0) quad (0.4w, 0.4w) → (0,r) quad (−0.4w, 0.4w) → (−r,0) quad (−0.4w,−0.4w) → (0,−r) close; restore.
shouldRepaint when simulation, progress or sprites identity change.
`_Atlas`: capacity `120·2 = 240` (an ember is two draws); preallocated Float32 transforms/rects (×4) and Int32 colours; paint filterQuality low. `add(canvas, image, f, center, extent, {color})`: flush if a different image or full; `scale = extent / image.width`; `scos = cos(rotation)·scale`, `ssin = sin(rotation)·scale`; RSTransform `[scos, ssin, cx − scos·w/2 + ssin·h/2, cy − ssin·w/2 − scos·h/2]` (rotate+scale about the image centre, centre placed on `center`); source rect full image; colour `(color ?? f.color) @ f.opacity` as ARGB. `flush`: `drawRawAtlas(image, transforms, rects, colors, BlendMode.modulate)` — white keeps the image's colours, another colour tints it, alpha fades it. Port: batch textured quads with per-instance tint = colour·opacity multiplied (modulate) into the texture.
`_Glyphs`: font size 64; fade quantized to 16 steps: `step = round(clamp(opacity)·16)`; cache key `(rgb & 0xFFFFFF) | (step << 24)`; TextPainter "?" in Inter (displayFamily) w800, size 64, height 1, colour `color @ step/16`, LTR, laid out once. `clear()` disposes all.

### lib/screens/channel/celebrations/particles/celebration_particles.dart

The deterministic particle engine. Constants: `kMaxCelebrationParticles = 120` (every preset stays under it whatever the duration; extra seeds are truncated with `sublist(0, 120)`); `kReducedCelebrationDuration = 360 ms`.
`CelebrationPalette`: lime #C8FF3D, mint #3DDBA6, green #12A87A, white #F5F5F5, orange #FF7A45 (heat only: flame and explosion).
`enum CelebrationSprite(asset, fallback)`: lime (spark_lime.png, lime), mint (spark_mint.png, mint), white (spark_white.png, white), dot (spark_dot.png, green). Declaration order matters (decode order, `values`).
`enum ParticleShape { sprite, rect, glow, dot, ring, arc, glyph }` — sprite (tintable spark image), rect (rounded confetto, `aspect` = height/width), glow (soft round glow), dot (filled ember with halo), ring (circle outline, size = radius), arc (part of outline from arcStart over arcSweep, size = radius), glyph ("?" in Inter, size = height).
`ParticleFrame` (immutable): shape, sprite?, color (default white), x, y (fractions of the area 0..1), size and stroke (fractions of the area's shorter side), aspect=1, rotation=0 (radians), flip=1 (−1..1 vertical squash), opacity, stroke=0, arcStart=0, arcSweep=0, depth=0.

`CelebrationSimulation(preset, duration, seed, reduceMotion=false)`: `_total = max(duration µs / 1e6, 0.1)` seconds; seeds built once from `math.Random(seed)` (Dart's PRNG — for bit-identical frames the Rust port must reimplement Dart VM's `_Random` (MWC with multiplier 0xFFFFDA61, seed scrambling as in sdk/lib/_internal/vm/lib/math_patch.dart, `nextDouble` = ((nextInt(1<<26) << 27) + nextInt(1<<27)) / 2^53, `nextInt` via rejection) and the overlay seed is Dart `String.hashCode` of the event id; otherwise any seeded PRNG keeps the same look, only the exact particle placement differs). The draw ORDER below must be kept for reproducibility. `particleCount` = number of seeds. `frameAt(progress)`: p = 0 if NaN else clamp 0..1; `seconds = p·total`; for each seed: `local = life <= 0 ? 1 : (seconds − start)/life`; skip if local < 0 or > 1; frame = reduceMotion ? `_still(seed, p)` (GLOBAL p) : `_frame(preset, seed, local, age = seconds − start)`; keep if non-null and opacity > 0.001. For starBonk, stable-sort by depth ascending. Pure function of (seed, duration, progress): any instant drawable without the previous, nothing accumulates.
`_Seed` fields: kind=0, sprite?, color=white, start, life (s), x=0.5, y=0.5, size=0.05, a,b,c,d=0.

Helpers: `_life(base, total) = min(base, 0.9·total)`; `_startIn(r, total, life) = rand·max(0, total − life)`; `_waveLife(total, waves, shortest, longest) = _life(clamp(total/waves·1.05, shortest, longest), total)`; `_wave(i, n, total, life) = n <= 1 ? 0 : i·max(0, total − life)/(n − 1)`; `_count(perSecond, total, min, max) = clamp(round(perSecond·total), min, max)`; `_between(r, a, b) = a + rand·(b − a)`; `_sparkCycle = [lime, white, mint]`.

### lib/screens/channel/hype_train/hype_train_math.dart

`enum HtUnlockState { locked, oneLevelAway, unlockNow, unlocked }`.
- `int percent(HtProgress p)`: goal <= 0 → 0; else `clamp(round(value/goal·100), 0, 100)` (in-level values). Dart `round()` is half away from zero.
- `bool isFinalLevel(train)`: `config.maxLevel != null && train.level >= maxLevel`.

- `List<(HtSharedChannel, int)> topShares(HtShared shared, {n = 3})`: channels with `progress.total > 0`; sum of totals; sum <= 0 → empty; sort descending by total (stable for ties); take n; share = `round(total/sum·100)` (shares may not sum to 100).

- `({int bits, int subs}) endTotals(List<HtTotal>)`: sums `quantity` for source bits and subs; other sources ignored.
- `_unlockState(level, threshold)`: level == threshold → unlockNow; > → unlocked; level+1 == threshold → oneLevelAway; else locked.
- `HtUnlockState? treasureState(train)`: null without treasure; else `_unlockState(level, treasure.threshold)`.

### lib/screens/channel/hype_train/hype_train_queue.dart

`class HtEventQueue` — realtime events waiting for the presenter (shown one at a time); bursts coalesce so the banner never falls far behind. `maxLength = 100` (oldest dropped beyond); `_burst = 5`; `ListQueue<HypeTrainEvent> _events`; `int? _mergeLevel`; `length`.
`enqueue(event)` (pattern switch, first match wins):
1. A small progression (`HtProgressionEvent(isLarge: false)`) when the queue already holds ≥5 events and the LAST queued one is also a small progression → replace the last with the new one (progress is cumulative) and return.
2. `HtConductorEvent`: if a queued conductor event has the same `conductor.source` (bits vs subs) → replace it in place (rebuild the queue) and return; else fall through to add.
3. `HtMergeEvent(train)`: remember `_mergeLevel = train.level`, then add.
4. `HtLevelUpEvent(progress, timeToExpire)` whose `progress.level.value == _mergeLevel` (the merge already announced this level) → instead add a synthetic `HtProgressionEvent(sequenceId: progress.total, action: HtAction('UNKNOWN'), source: unknown, quantity: 0, isLarge: false, progress, expiresAt: timeToExpire)` and return.
5. Otherwise add.
`_add`: addLast, then drop from the front while length > 100. `pop()`: removeFirst or null. `drainForEnd()`: list of all queued (oldest first), then `clear()`. `clear()`: empties and resets `_mergeLevel`.

### lib/screens/channel/hype_train/hype_train_reducer.dart

`enum HtAthTransition { none, approaching, reached }`.
`HtApplyResult` (immutable): `train` (after; identical to the input when ignored), `applied = true`, `ignoredReason?`, `levelChangedFrom?` (previous level when changed), `athTransition = none`, `treasureUnlockedNow = false`, `communityUnlockedNow = false`, `deferredNextLevel = false` (a next-level progression arrived before its level-up: wait for the level-up then apply with force), `endTotals = []`, `endRewards = []`, `mergedFrom?` (progress before a merge — where the level pill counts from), `previousChannels = []`, `joiningChannels = []`, `continuesIn?` (where a shared train continues after this channel left). `HtApplyResult.ignored(train, reason)`: applied false, everything else default.

`applyEvent(train, event, {required Set<String> seenIds, String? channelId, bool force = false})` — pure except `seenIds` (collects `trainId:sequenceId` of applied progressions to drop redeliveries):
- Progression → `_progression`.
- LevelUp → `_levelUp`.
- Conductor: copy the list; replace the conductor of the same source or INSERT AT FRONT if new; result with new conductors.
- End: `copyWith(endReason: event.reason, endedAt: event.endedAt)` (endedAt null keeps old), `endTotals`, `endRewards`.
- Start: same id as current → ignored 'duplicate start' (a late/repeated start must not reset the running train); else result = the new train.
- Merge: result `event.train`, `levelChangedFrom: event.train.level != train.level ? train.level : null`, `mergedFrom: event.previousProgress ?? train.progress`, previous/joining channels.
- ChannelLeft: `shouldEnd` → `copyWith(endReason: completed)`; else result(train, continuesIn).
- Approaching → ignored 'approaching'; Ignored/Unknown → ignored 'not applicable'.
`_progression`: key = `'${event.trainId ?? train.id}:${event.sequenceId}'`; in seenIds → ignored 'duplicate'. `sequenceId < train.progress.total` → ignored 'stale' (equal is NOT stale: the level-up may have come first with the same total and the progression still names the contributor). level = event level; `nextLevel = level == train.level + 1`; nextLevel && !force → `HtApplyResult(train, applied: false, deferredNextLevel: true)`. `level != train.level && !nextLevel` → ignored 'level mismatch'. Add key to seenIds. shared = `_withSharedProgress(train.shared, event.sharedProgress)`; own = channelId != null ? sharedProgress[channelId] : null; progress = own == null ? event.progress : event.progress with `athState: own.athState`. after = `copyWith(progress, expiresAt: event.expiresAt, treasure: event.treasure, shared)` (null arguments keep the previous values — copyWith uses `??`). Return `_withTransitions(train, after, levelChangedFrom: nextLevel ? train.level : null)`.
`_levelUp`: level < train.level or `progress.total < train.progress.total` → ignored 'stale'. `fresh = event.train` (the level-up carries the whole train in GQL shape: refreshes variant details, shared channels, config). progress = event.progress; if fresh and channelId: for each shared channel whose user id == channelId with progress, set athState from it. after = copyWith(progress, expiresAt: timeToExpire, config/ath/variant/treasure/community/mythic/shared from fresh (null keeps), conductors: fresh's if non-empty). `_withTransitions(..., levelChangedFrom: level != train.level ? train.level : null)`.
`_withTransitions(before, after, {levelChangedFrom})`: athTransition from before/after athState; `treasureUnlockedNow = levelChanged && treasure != null && after.level == treasure.threshold`; `communityUnlockedNow` likewise with community.threshold.
`_athTransition(before, after)`: reached(s) = reached or justReached. reached(after) && !reached(before) → reached; after == approaching && before == none → approaching; else none.
`_withSharedProgress(shared, progress)`: null if either null; else same id, each channel's progress replaced by `progress[user.id] ?? channel.progress` (ath, conductors kept).

### lib/screens/channel/hype_train/hype_train_sequence.dart

Turns one applied event into a list of timed banner steps (the presenter plays them in order).
`enum HtStepKind` (declaration order): fadeIn, fadeOut, progress (resting state: level, bar, timer), fillTo, flash100, marquee, instruction, levelComplete, sharedAvatars, sharedTop3, largeContribution, coinUnlock, rocketLaunch, unlockIcon (still coin/rocket, reduced motion), mythicPool, mythicSelect, mythicReveal, athReached, athOtherChannel, mergeAura, channelLeftSummary, continuesIn, totals, summary, rewardsStrip, expiredStrip.
`HtStep(kind, duration, {data = {}, rainbow = false})` — immutable; `data` map keys used: `percent`, `level`, `emotes`, `text`, `treasure`, `community`, `channels`, `channel`, `shares`, `event`, `previous`, `joining`, `fromLevel`, `toLevel`, `totals`, `ath`, `selected`, `emote`, `style`, `icon`, `static`. `rainbow` = the shared/record rainbow sweep over the step. `isStrip` = rewardsStrip or expiredStrip. `copyWith`. `Duration total(steps)` = sum of durations.
`HtPlanInput(event, after, result, before?, channelId?, compact=false, reduceMotion=false, presenterVisible=true)` — compact = collapsed pill or landscape (only level pill and strips); presenterVisible false = off screen (steps take no time, strips skipped).

`planSequence(input)`: steps = `_eventSteps + _athSteps`; reduce motion → each through `_still`; compact → `_compact`; not visible → every non-strip step with duration 0 (strips REMOVED). Order of transforms matters (still, then compact, then visibility).
Helpers: `_fillTo(v)` = fillTo, 200 ms, {percent: v}; `_instruction(text, extra)` = instruction, 2000 ms, {text, ...extra}; `_rainbowMarquee(cycle, data)` = marquee, duration cycle·3, rainbow true; `_sharedTop3(train)` = sharedTop3, 1000 + 2500 = 3500 ms, {shares: topShares(shared)}.

`_eventSteps` by event:
- Start, shared train: [fadeIn 500 ms; sharedAvatars 6000 ms {channels: users of all shared channels}; instruction 'start' 2000 ms].
- Start, otherwise: [fadeIn 500; marquee 3000 ms {emotes: mythic ? mythic.pool (or []) : [calloutEmote ×3 (each omitted if null)]}; instruction 'start'; then instruction 'treasureReachLevel' {level: treasure.threshold} if treasure, else 'communityReachLevel' {level: community.threshold} if community; progress 0 ms].
- Progression: if isLarge → [fadeOut 500, largeContribution 6000 {event}, fadeIn 500]; then always fillTo(percent(after.progress)).
- LevelUp → `_levelUp`.
- Merge: [mergeAura 1000 ms {previous: previousChannels, joining: joiningChannels}; marquee 3000 ms {fromLevel: mergedFrom.level ?? levelChangedFrom ?? before.level, toLevel: after.level}; progress 0].
- ChannelLeft: next = result.continuesIn ?? event.continuesIn; [channelLeftSummary 2000 ms; next != null ? continuesIn 3000 ms {channel: next} : instruction 3000 ms {text: 'seeYouNextTime'}] (note this instruction uses scrollLong 3000 ms, not textShort).
- End: completed → `_completed`; otherwise [expiredStrip 20000 ms].
- Conductor / Approaching / Ignored / Unknown → [].
`_levelUp`: after.level < 2 → []. from = levelChangedFrom; null → [fillTo(percent)]. completed data = {emotes: before.progress.level.rewards ?? [], level: from}. Steps: fillTo(100); flash100 500 ms; shared ? rainbowMarquee(2000 → 6000 ms total, completed) : marquee 2500 ms completed; levelComplete 500 ms {level: from}; shared → sharedTop3; if treasureUnlockedNow → [coinUnlock 1600 ms, marquee 3000 {treasure: discountPercentage}, instruction 'treasureUnlocked'] else if treasureState == oneLevelAway → instruction 'treasureOneAway' {level: threshold}; if communityUnlockedNow → [rocketLaunch 1600, marquee 3000 {community: community.current}, instruction 'communityUnlocked']; if mythic → [mythicPool 2500 {emotes: pool}, instruction 'mythicPool']; finally fillTo(percent(after.progress)).
`_completed`: ath = after.progress.athState reached/justReached; shared; rewards = {emotes: result.endRewards}. Steps: ath ? rainbowMarquee(4200 → 12600 ms) : shared ? rainbowMarquee(4500 → 13500 ms) : marquee 3000 rewards; mythic → [mythicSelect 2000 {emotes: pool, selected: selectedEmote}, mythicReveal 2000 {emote: selectedEmote}]; community → instruction 'communityRewards'; if the end event is NOT synthesized (our own end has no totals) → totals 6000 ms {totals: endTotals(result.endTotals)}; shared → sharedTop3; summary 2000 ms {ath, level: after.level}; rewardsStrip 20000 ms {emotes: endRewards}.
`_athSteps` by result.athTransition: none → []; approaching → [fadeIn 500 {style: 'ath'}]; reached → [athReached 6000 ms] + for each shared channel of AFTER that is not this channelId, whose athState is reached now and was not before (before map from `before.shared.channels`) → athOtherChannel 6000 {channel: user}.
`_still(step)` (reduce motion): marquee → data + {static: true}, rainbow false (duration kept); coinUnlock → unlockIcon 1000 ms {icon: 'coin'}; rocketLaunch → unlockIcon 1000 {icon: 'rocket'}; anything else with rainbow → rainbow false; else unchanged.
`_compact(input, steps)`: level-up event → if from null or after.level < 2 → []; else [levelComplete 2500 ms {level: from}] (single step); other events → only the strip steps.

### lib/screens/channel/hype_train/stores/hype_train_store.dart

MobX store, one per channel screen (`HypeTrainStore = HypeTrainStoreBase with _$HypeTrainStore`; generated mixin not ported literally). GQL snapshot on join (and on resubscribe, app resume, while realtime is down), realtime events presented one at a time with the official timings, plus approaching, end and expiry flows. Anonymous; can never affect chat or video.

Enums: `HtPhase { hidden, approaching, ongoing, ending, rewards, expired }`; `HtStyle { regular, community, treasure, shared, mythic, goldenKappa, athReached, athApproaching }` (banner look). `HtEndSummary(reason, level, bits, subs, rewards = [], ath = false)` (ath = beat the channel's record).

Constructor `(channelId, service: TwitchHypeTrainService, realtime, viewerId?: String? Function() (default returns null), serverClock?)`: subscribes to realtime topic `hype-train-events-v2.<channelId>` and listens; on throw (malformed id) logs and sets `_realtimeDegraded = true` (GQL alone). Then `_refetch()`.
Private constants: `_minRefetchGap = 2 s`, `_pollInterval = 20 s`, `_levelUpWait = 3 s`, `_approachingGrace = 5 s`, `_countdownTick = 1 s`.
Private state: `_queue` (HtEventQueue), `_seenIds`, subscription + listener, `_presenters` count, `_compact`, `_reduceMotion`, `_busy`, `_steps`, `_cursor`, `_stepTimer`, `_deferred` (next-level progression waiting for its level-up) + `_deferredTimer`, `_countdown`, `_expiryTimer`, `_approachingTicker`, `_stripTicker`, `_pollTimer`, `_fetching`, `_lastFetch`, `_refetchPending`, `_refetchTimer`, `_queuedExpiry` (latest expiry among queued events — a queued level-up extends the train before being presented), `_endSynthesized` (the end on screen is our expiry fallback), `_failures` (public getter `failures`), `_deadlines` (Map<int, DateTime>), `_approachingSince`, `_stripStart`, `_disposed`, `_inBackground`, `_onSequenceDone`. Test hooks: `appliedEvents` counter, `queuedCount`, `injectEvent(event)`.

Observables (`@readonly` unless noted): `phase` (hidden), `train`, `approaching`, `step` (on screen; null at rest), `stepIndex`, `shownLevel` (the pill's level: old one until "level complete" shows), `shownPercent` (follows fillTo steps, not data), `remaining` (Duration), `approachingFraction` (0→1 from arrival to last deadline), `approachingChecks` (filled check circles: deadline keys 1 and 2 still ahead), `approachingEventsLeft` (smallest deadline key ahead), `approachingCompleted` (a train event arrived: bar drawn full), `approachingNMore` (viewer already took part: "N more"), `stripFraction` (0→1 over the 20 s end strip), `endSummary`, `unlockedRewards` (emotes earned this session), `realtimeDegraded`; plain `@observable expanded = false`, `collapsed = false`.
Computeds: `percent` (htm.percent of train progress or 0), `level`, `visible` (phase != hidden), `isFinalLevel`, `levelTarget`, `style` (priority: athState approaching → athApproaching; reached/justReached → athReached; variant goldenKappa → goldenKappa; mythic → mythic; shared != null → shared; treasure → treasure; community → community; else regular), `summaryTime` (seconds = remaining.inSeconds; ≥60 → (minutes: ceil(s/60), seconds: 0) else (0, s) — computed so readers rebuild once a minute until the last minute).

Presenter/lifecycle: `attachPresenter()` (counted), `updatePresentation({compact, reduceMotion})` (applies to the next sequence), `reduceMotion` getter, `detachPresenter()` (count min 0; when it hits 0 while busy → `_finishSequenceNow()` so the store never stalls in the background), `toggleExpanded()`, `setCollapsed(v)`, `handleAppBackground()` (flag + `_updatePolling`), `handleAppResume()` (clear flag, polling, `_refetch()`).
`loadUpcomingRewards()` → `service.getUpcomingRewards(channelId)` cached per train id (List<(level, rewards)>; sheet's "Coming up"); no train → []. `loadPinata()` → `service.getOngoingPinata(channelId)` cached per train id; a synchronous throw → completed null. `dispose()` idempotent: cancel all timers (step, deferred, countdown, expiry, approaching, strip, poll, refetch), listener, subscription.

Realtime `_onTopicEvent`: Message → observe serverTime, `_onEvent(HypeTrainEvent.parse(payload))`; Subscribed → realtimeDegraded false, polling, refetch if resubscribe; Unavailable → degraded true, polling.

`_onEvent(event)` (action):
1. Phase ending/rewards/expired: if (Start of another train id) OR (Approaching while phase != ending) → `_toHidden()` and continue (a new train cuts the end short). Else: if `_endSynthesized` and the event is a Progression or LevelUp → `_refetch()` (our expiry guess was wrong; GQL brings it back); return.
2. eventTrainId = progression/end `trainId`, or levelUp's `train.id`; if phase ongoing, a current train, and eventTrainId differs → `_refetch()`, return (ours is stale; GQL and realtime share train ids).
3. End event → if train and ongoing `_beginEnd(event)`; return.
4. Ongoing: approaching events ignored; else `_noteQueuedExpiry(event)`, enqueue, `_pump()`; return.
5. Hidden/approaching: isTrainEvent = Start/Progression/LevelUp/Conductor/Merge; if approaching and train event → `approachingCompleted = true`, `approachingFraction = 1`. Then: Approaching → `_showApproaching`; Start or Merge → cancel approaching ticker (the full bar stays readable while fading into the start; cleared when the train ends), phase ongoing, enqueue, pump; LevelUp carrying a train → apply it to its own fresh train, `_clearApproaching()`, phase ongoing, `_setTrain(result.train)` (shown as is, no animation); other Progression/LevelUp/Conductor/ChannelLeft → `_refetch()` (we missed the start); End/Ignored/Unknown → nothing.

Presentation: `_pump()` loops while not busy: next = `_takeDeferredIfReady()` (deferred whose level equals the train's current level; cancels its timer) ?? queue.pop(); `_present(event)`.
`_present(event)` (action): no train → only Start/Merge produce `HtApplyResult(train)`, others dropped. Else `applyEvent(before, event, seenIds, channelId)`. If deferredNextLevel for a progression → store it in `_deferred`, arm 3 s timer that drops it and refetches; return. Not applied → refetch if reason 'level mismatch' (lost events); return. `appliedEvents++`. New train id → clear seenIds, unlockedRewards, queuedExpiry. If level changed → append the previous level's rewards of type emote to `unlockedRewards`. `_setTrain(result.train, animated: true)`. If the deferred progression's level equals the new level → cancel its timer (it follows this sequence). If train endReason completed and the event is ChannelLeft → `_beginEnd(HtEndEvent(reason: completed))`; return. Steps = `planSequence(HtPlanInput(event, before, after, result, channelId, compact, reduceMotion, presenterVisible: presenters > 0))`. `shownLevel = (levelChangedFrom != null && steps contain levelComplete) ? from : train.level`. If it was the first train → `shownPercent = percent(progress)`. `_runSteps(steps)`.
`_runSteps(steps, {onDone})`: cancel step timer, busy, set steps, cursor 0, onDone, `_advance()`.
`_advance()` loop: past the end → step null, not busy, if ongoing `_showTrainAsIs()`, call+clear onDone, `_pump()`, return. Else step = steps[cursor]; set `step`, `stepIndex`; if the previous step was levelComplete → shownLevel = train.level (the pill flips after "level complete"). fillTo with int percent → shownPercent = value; flash100 → shownPercent = 100. Strip → `_startStrip(step)`. Zero duration → cursor++ and continue; else `Timer(duration)` → cursor++, advance.
`_finishSequenceNow()`: cancel step timer; remaining steps from the cursor without strips, durations 0; cursor 0; cancel strip ticker; advance (applies state instantly).
`_setTrain(train, {animated=false})`: set; if not animated `_showTrainAsIs()`; `_updateCountdown()`, `_scheduleExpiry()`, `_updatePolling()`. `_showTrainAsIs()`: shownLevel = level ?? 0, shownPercent = percent.
Countdown: only while ongoing with expiresAt; `_tick()` immediately and `Timer.periodic(1 s)`; `_tick`: `left = expiresAt − serverClock.now()`; `seconds = ceil(left.ms/1000)` (rounded up like the official countdown); `remaining = max(0, seconds) s`. Otherwise cancel and remaining 0.
Expiry (decision D5: Twitch sometimes never sends the end): `_noteQueuedExpiry(event)` takes levelUp.timeToExpire or progression.expiresAt; if later than `_queuedExpiry ?? train.expiresAt` → record and reschedule. `_scheduleExpiry()`: expiresAt = later of queued and train expiry; only while ongoing; timer at `expiresAt + 10 s − serverNow` (min 0) → if still ongoing with a train, `_onEvent(HtEndEvent(reason: level >= 2 ? completed : expired, synthesized: true))`.
`_beginEnd(end)` (action): cancel step timer, not busy, clear onDone, cancel deferred timer, take deferred, clear queuedExpiry, `_endSynthesized = end.synthesized`. Apply silently (force: true) the deferred then every drained queued event (whatever is queued already happened), counting applied ones. `result = applyEvent(train, end)`; set train; cancel countdown and expiry. Totals via `htm.endTotals`; ath from athState; `endSummary = HtEndSummary(reason, level, bits, subs, rewards: endRewards, ath)`; if endRewards non-empty → unlockedRewards = endRewards. Plan steps; `phase = completed ? ending : expired`; remaining 0; polling; `_runSteps(steps, onDone: _toHidden)`.
`_startStrip(step)`: phase = rewardsStrip ? rewards : expired; `_stripStart = clock.now()`, fraction 0; `Timer.periodic(500 ms)` setting `stripFraction = min(1, elapsed ms / step.duration ms)` (20 s).
`_toHidden()` (action): cancel step/strip/countdown/expiry/deferred timers; reset busy, onDone, steps, queue, queuedExpiry, endSynthesized; phase hidden; train null; step null; strip start/fraction 0; remaining 0; unlockedRewards []; `expanded = false`; `_clearApproaching()`; polling.
Approaching `_showApproaching(a)` (action): `now = serverNow`; deadlines = each `events → now + seconds`; empty → return. `arriving = phase != approaching`; `first = deadlines[1] ?? latest deadline`; if arriving and `first − now <= 20 s` → return (too late to be worth showing; updates to a banner already up always apply). Set approaching, deadlines; if arriving → since = now, completed false, fraction 0. `approachingNMore = viewerId != null && participants contains it`. phase approaching; `_approachingTick()` and restart a `Timer.periodic(500 ms)`; polling.
`_approachingTick()` (action): if phase != approaching cancel ticker, return. checks = count of keys {1, 2} whose deadline is after now; eventsLeft = min key ahead, or `approaching.goal` (default 3) if none ahead; last = latest deadline; since = approachingSince ?? now; `span = last − since` ms; if not completed and span > 0 → fraction = clamp(elapsed/span). If now > last + 5 s → `_toHidden()` and `_refetch()` (no start came; GQL may bring it back).
`_clearApproaching()`: cancel ticker; approaching null; deadlines {}; since null; checks/eventsLeft/fraction 0; completed/nMore false.
GQL `_refetch()`: single-flight (sets `_refetchPending` if fetching); at most every 2 s (otherwise one trailing timer `_refetchTimer ??=` at the end of the gap); `service.getHypeTrain(channelId)`; on success failures 0 and `_applySnapshot`; on any error failures++ and keep what is shown; finally if pending → run again.
`_applySnapshot(snapshot)` (action):
- `HtOngoingSnapshot(train: fetched)`: if phase ending/rewards/expired → if same id and the end was Twitch's (not synthesized) → return (Twitch's end stands); else `_toHidden()`. If same id as current and `fetched.total < current.total` → return (lagging). If `progress.remainingSeconds` ≥ 0 → `expiresAt = serverNow + remainingSeconds`. If ongoing, same id and equal total → refresh details only (`train = …`, `_showTrainAsIs()` if not busy, countdown, expiry) and return. Otherwise show it as it is now (no start animation; queued events are older): cancel step timer, not busy, clear onDone, step null, clear queue, queuedExpiry, deferred + timer; new id → clear seenIds and unlockedRewards; `_clearApproaching()`; phase ongoing; `_setTrain(train)`; `_pump()`.
- `HtApproachingSnapshot` → `_showApproaching` only if phase hidden or approaching.
- `HtNoTrain` → only while ongoing: if `expiresAt + 10 s` is still in the future the end is on its way (or our fallback will present it) → keep; else `_toHidden()`.
Polling (decision D12) `_updatePolling()`: active = ongoing or approaching; if realtime degraded && active && not disposed && not background → `Timer.periodic(20 s, refetch)` (`??=`); else cancel. Debug log `HypeTrainStore(<channelId>) <message>` in debug builds only.

### lib/screens/channel/hype_train/widgets/hype_train_split_color.dart

The "text turns colour exactly at the fill edge" mechanism (dark text over the fill, light text over the track).
`HtFillScope(fraction: ValueListenable<double>, child)` → `RenderHtFillScope` (RenderProxyBox): holds `fraction` (0–1 of its width filled, as drawn — includes the bar's animation) and `textDirection`; keeps a set of registered `RenderHtSplitColor` descendants; setting a new fraction listenable re-subscribes every split; a text direction change repaints them. `edge` (in scope coordinates) = `width·clamp(fraction)` for LTR, `width − filled` for RTL.

`HtSplitColor(onFill, onTrack, child)` → `RenderHtSplitColor`: on attach walks up the render tree to the nearest `RenderHtFillScope`, registers, and listens to its fraction (repaint on change); detach unregisters. `alwaysNeedsCompositing` when it has a child; layer is a `ShaderMaskLayer`. `split` (visible for tests) → `(at, fillOnLeft)`: no scope / no size / zero width → (ltr ? 0 : 1, ltr); else transform own left (0,0) and right (width,0) into scope coordinates; span = right − left (0 → (0, ltr)); `at = clamp((scope.edge − left)/span, 0, 1)`, fillOnLeft = ltr. Without a scope it paints everything in onTrack (at=0 with fill on left). Port: render the text twice with two clip rects split at the edge x (fill colour left of the edge, track colour right; mirrored for RTL), or a two-colour shader mask.

### lib/screens/channel/hype_train/widgets/effects/rainbow_sweep.dart

`RainbowSweep(cycleDuration, child, cycles = 3)` — Twitch's shared-train/record rainbow: a top-to-bottom 4-colour gradient behind `child`, filling it. Colours (`RainbowSweep.colors`): pink #FA1ED2, lilac #F093F9, orange #ED722F, yellow #FAF31A. Over each quarter of a cycle every stop slides linearly to the next colour; `cycles` cycles play then it rests on the final frame (end of quarter 3 → colours shifted by 4 ≡ original order). Text over it reads in black (the marquee is restyled black without shadows by the caller).
State `RainbowSweepState`: AnimationController(cycleDuration), status listener: on completed `_done++`, if `_done < cycles` → `forward(from: 0)`; started in initState. Test getters `isAnimating`, `gradient`.
`gradient`: `position = clamp(value·4, 0, 4)`; `quarter = clamp(floor(position), 0, 3)`; `t = position − quarter`; colours[k] = `lerp(colors[(k+quarter) % 4], colors[(k+quarter+1) % 4], t)` for k = 0..3 (linear RGB lerp, evenly spaced default stops), begin topCenter, end bottomCenter.
Build: Stack(passthrough): `Positioned.fill(IgnorePointer(AnimatedBuilder → DecoratedBox(key 'rainbow', gradient)))`, then child.
Used for steps with `rainbow: true` (shared level-up: cycle 2000 ms; shared completed: 4500 ms; record completed: 4200 ms), never under reduce motion.

### lib/screens/channel/hype_train/widgets/effects/trophy_burst.dart

`TrophyBurst(duration, reduceMotion = false)` — a new all-time high: the gold trophy pops in elastically over a glow, with a repeating ring pulse. Used in the athReached step (6000 ms) via `_effectRow`.
